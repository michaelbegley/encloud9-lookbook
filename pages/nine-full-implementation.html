What does a full Dynamics 365 implementation include?

A full implementation covers everything in your signed scope: configuration of Dynamics 365 Sales, Customer Service or both, data moved from your current CRM or spreadsheets, the integrations you name, training and go-live. Anything not named in the scope, agents and Microsoft licenses are not part of it.

Included

  • A written scope from a paid Fit Check, with assumptions and exclusions
  • Configuration of Sales, and Customer Service if in scope
  • Data migration from your current CRM or spreadsheets
  • Integrations named in the scope
  • Your Microsoft licenses assigned to each user
  • Training, go-live and handover to a support plan

Not included

  • Anything not named in the signed scope
  • Agents and Copilot Studio work, scoped separately
  • Microsoft licenses and Copilot Credits

Anything outside the package is a written change order you approve before work starts.

Is a full implementation right for your business?

A full implementation fits a business that needs other systems connected, or data moved from several systems, when Dynamics 365 goes live. It is more than you need if this is your first Dynamics 365 Sales or Customer Service system and your process is standard; an accelerator fits that better.

How does a full Dynamics 365 implementation run?

Every project runs through the same four stages: Plan, Build, Go live and Run. You see the written plan before we build, and every change after that is written down and approved.

Stage APlanWe learn how you sell, serve and bill, using a written discovery questionnaire, and give you the plan: apps, users, data, and where Copilot fits.
Stage BBuildWe build Dynamics 365 to the plan, using standard features first, and move your data. You check the imported records against your old system before sign-off.
Stage CGo liveTraining and cut-over day. Copilot is switched on if your plan includes it; agents are scoped separately.
Stage DRunYour support plan starts. Health checks and updates are logged, so you always know what changed.

How much does a full Dynamics 365 implementation cost?

A full implementation is quoted in writing as a fixed price per phase, after a paid Fit Check. Microsoft licenses are a separate cost: Microsoft's list price is $65, $105 or $150 per user per month, paid yearly, for Sales Professional, Enterprise or Premium. Support plans start at $500 a month.

How long does a Dynamics 365 implementation take?

A full implementation typically takes [Brian sets] to [Brian sets] months. You get the plan, with dates, before we build. On our 9 published projects with a delivery time, go-live took 1 to 6 months, and 7 of the 9 took three months or less.

What is the difference between an accelerator and a full implementation?

Start from where your system is today: a first Dynamics 365 Sales or Customer Service system fits an accelerator, and a system that needs integrations or data from several systems on day one fits a full implementation. A Dynamics 365 system that is over-customized or stalled fits Rescue and Repair.

What is the paid Fit Check?

The Fit Check is the paid first step of every full implementation: we review your current system and needs, then give you a written scope and a fixed quote for the build. The quote is yours to keep, whether or not you build with us.

Can you move our data from Salesforce, HubSpot or spreadsheets?

Yes. Moving data from your old CRM or spreadsheets is part of a full implementation, and the integrations you name are part of the written scope and its fixed price per phase.

Are Microsoft licenses included?

No. Microsoft licenses and Copilot Credits are not part of the package price, and you pay for them separately. As part of the build we assign your licenses to each user and set their security roles, because Microsoft does not let a user open the app until both are done.

What if our scope changes during the project?

Anything outside the package is a written change order you approve before work starts. Each phase has its own fixed price, so you approve the cost of a change before it is built.

What are the phases of a Dynamics 365 implementation?

Microsoft's Success by Design framework uses five phases: Discover, Initiate, Implement, Prepare and Operate. Our four stages, Plan, Build, Go live and Run, follow the same path, starting with a written scope from the Fit Check.

Do we need a support plan after go-live?

Yes. New clients start on at least the Stability plan, $500 a month for 4 hours, on a 12-month agreement. Monthly plans include a 1-hour response during business hours, 7:00am to 5:00pm Central, Monday to Friday.
