What was wrong with JASCO's CRM?

JASCO, Inc. makes analytical instruments, specializing in chromatography and molecular spectroscopy. It operates in 57 countries and serves manufacturers and research labs.

JASCO was struggling with an outdated on-premises Dynamics 365 system. It needed a modern CRM to improve efficiency and streamline its processes.

What did we change?

We implemented Dynamics 365 Sales, including a Lead Assignment Rule Table with over 900 rules, and updated JASCO's form captures.

This automated lead assignments and improved data management.

What changed day to day?

JASCO now has a centralized, automated system for sales and lead management, which has greatly improved efficiency and customer interaction.

What comes next for JASCO?

JASCO continues to work with us on ongoing enhancements, including potential integrations such as Adobe Sign and international lead management.
