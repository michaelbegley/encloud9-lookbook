When does a business need Project Operations?

When you sell time and expertise: consulting, engineering, agencies or professional services. Project Operations follows a job from the quote through staffing and time to the invoice.

What is Dynamics 365 Project Operations?

Microsoft's app for project-based businesses. It covers project quotes, staffing, plans, time and expenses, and billing in one system.

How much does Project Operations cost?

Project Operations is $135 per user per month, paid yearly. Copilot Credits are not included. Staff who only enter time and expenses can use the lower-cost Team Members license.

What does Copilot do in Project Operations?

Copilot drafts task plans, risk assessments and status reports, so project managers start from a draft instead of a blank page.

Does it work with Dynamics 365 Sales?

Yes. A deal won in Dynamics 365 Sales can become a project, so the quote and the customer carry over without retyping.

What does setup cost?

Our build work is a fixed price, quoted in writing after a short call. Microsoft licenses are a separate cost, and support plans start at $500 a month.
