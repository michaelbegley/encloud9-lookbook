Microsoft's four steps

Don't ignore it: four steps from warning to lockout

Microsoft is rolling out staged limits for Dataverse storage. Today only the warning emails are on. Once the rollout reaches your tenant, here is what happens when you go over.

  1. 85%Past 85%The warnings start. Once you are near or over the limit, your admins get them every week, with no way to switch them off.
  2. 100%The day you go overYou cannot create, copy, restore or recover environments. That includes restoring an environment from a backup if something goes wrong.
  3. 30 days30 days overYour sandbox environments lock to administrators only.
  4. 90 days90 days overNobody can sign in to those sandboxes, admins included.

Your production apps keep running, but anything that needs a restore, a copy or a test environment is blocked.

Source: Microsoft, "Dataverse capacity-based storage details" (read 6 November 2026). The staged limits apply to Dataverse-only tenants as Microsoft rolls out its new storage validation; sandboxes on pay-as-you-go billing are not locked.

What happens when we go over our Dataverse storage limit?

Once Microsoft's rollout reaches your tenant: from the day you go over, you cannot create, copy, restore or recover environments. After 30 days, sandbox environments are limited to administrators. After 90 days, nobody can sign in to them, admins included.

What messages will we see from Microsoft?

Warning emails, plus banners in the Power Platform admin center, the maker portals and your Dynamics 365 apps. They start when less than 15% of your database, file or log storage is left, get stronger below 5%, and continue once you are over. Admins and makers get the emails every week.

Will Dynamics 365 CRM stop working?

No. Your production apps keep running. What is blocked is anything that needs a restore, a copy or a test environment.

Can we turn off Microsoft's storage warning emails or notifications?

No. Once you are near or over the limit, admins get the emails every week, with no way to opt out. A banner you dismiss stays hidden for seven days, then comes back if you are still over.

Which storage type is my warning about?

Dataverse storage splits into database, file and log. You can see the split in the Power Platform admin center under Licensing, then Dataverse. The free analysis checks this for you.

How much does extra Dataverse storage cost?

Extra database storage can cost up to $500 per GB a year.

Should we buy more storage or clean up our data?

It depends which storage type is over and what is using it. Database storage is expensive, so a cleanup often pays for itself. The analysis recommends one or both.

What takes up the most Dataverse storage?

Usually old emails and attachments, notes, audit history, system logs and environments nobody uses. The analysis finds the biggest tables and the fastest-growing ones.
