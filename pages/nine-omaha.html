Is enCloud9 based in Omaha?

Yes. Our office is at 2915 Lynnwood Dr., Bellevue, NE 68123, in the Omaha metro. We work with clients in Nebraska, Iowa and across the US.

Do you work on site or remotely?

Mostly remotely, through Teams and screen sharing. For Omaha-area clients, we visit on site when the work needs it.

What Dynamics 365 work do you do?

We build new Dynamics 365 systems, rescue ones that are not working, run fixed-scope accelerators for Sales and Customer Service, and support systems after go-live.

How long does a Dynamics 365 project take?

A full implementation or a Rescue and Repair usually takes 8 to 16 weeks and is quoted for your project. A fixed-scope accelerator is shorter.

How long have you worked with your clients?

enCloud9 has been a Microsoft Partner since 2008. Clinical Reference Lab (CRL) has been a client for more than 15 years.

Do you have Omaha clients?

Yes. One is Radio Engineering Industries in Omaha, which runs its five divisions on Dynamics 365.
