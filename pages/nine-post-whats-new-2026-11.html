What Changed in Dynamics 365 in November 2026

The short answerIn November 2026, Customer Insights added reusable segment templates and business unit access control. From November 15, Power Platform stops creating new unmanaged developer environments and Dataverse for Teams environments. On November 30, record-level copresence and Power Automate’s Describe it to design it retire. Release Planner retires by November 15.

This is enCloud9’s monthly roundup of what changed in Dynamics 365 and the Power Platform it runs on. Every item links to the Microsoft page it came from, checked 10 November 2026.

Microsoft no longer publishes twice-yearly release plans, so changes now arrive month by month. Read how Microsoft updates Dynamics 365 now for the background.

What is new in Customer Insights - Journeys?

The November 2026 update of Customer Insights - Journeys (release 1.3.2529.71) brings three changes (checked 10 November 2026):

  • Reusable segment templates, generally available. Pick a built-in template, such as event attendance or email engagement, or save your own segment query as a template.
  • A customizable consent list in double opt-in emails. You choose how purposes and topics appear when people confirm their choices.
  • Named, color-coded segment groups. Complex segments are easier to review at a glance.

What is new in Customer Insights - Data?

Two features became generally available in November 2026 (checked 10 November 2026):

  • Admins can show or hide columns and tables in the segment and measure builders, so users see only the data they need.
  • Admins can control access to customer profiles, segments and measures by business unit.

What changes in Power Platform on November 15?

Two kinds of environment can no longer be created from November 15, 2026 (checked 10 November 2026).

Unmanaged developer environments. Microsoft deprecated them. Existing ones are suspended starting December 15, 2026 and deleted starting January 15, 2027. Developer environments that are managed environments aren’t affected. Each user can now create one managed developer environment.

Dataverse for Teams environments. These are deprecated too. Existing ones keep working until September 15, 2027, when any not upgraded to Dataverse are suspended. They are decommissioned on October 30, 2027.

To find them, open the Power Platform admin center and go to Manage, then Environments. Microsoft says to filter where Type is Developer and Managed is No.

What retires on November 30?

  • Record-level copresence in model-driven apps. Users will no longer see who else is working on the same record. Record sharing isn’t affected.
  • Describe it to design it in Power Automate. Makers can no longer create cloud flows by describing them. Existing flows aren’t deleted or moved.

Both dates were checked 10 November 2026 on Microsoft Learn.

What changed in Project Operations?

The November 2026 Project Operations release adds the following (checked 10 November 2026):

  • A Policy Authoring Assistant, an agent that reviews the policy documents behind the Activity Approvals Agent
  • A full-screen mode for the task grid
  • Weekly Time Entry Notes, in preview
  • New version numbering from version 4.173, which Microsoft says changes numbering only

Where does Microsoft announce Dynamics 365 changes now?

On the AI at Work roadmap, formerly the Microsoft 365 Roadmap.

Microsoft told partners in September 2026 that it is moving from twice-yearly release waves to continuous roadmap communications. It is archiving its release plans on Microsoft Learn and retiring Release Planner by November 15, 2026 (checked 10 November 2026).

Microsoft says the change affects roadmap communications only. Release schedules, deployment, Message Center notices and documentation stay the same.

What should you do this month?

  • Find any unmanaged developer environments and convert them or export their solutions before December 15.
  • Tell makers that Describe it to design it retires on November 30.
  • Bookmark the AI at Work roadmap, and update any links to Release Planner.
  • If you use Customer Insights, try segment templates on your next campaign.

Frequently asked questions

Can we still create a developer environment after November 15, 2026?

Only a managed developer environment, one per user. New unmanaged developer environments can’t be created from November 15, 2026.

What happens to existing unmanaged developer environments?

Microsoft suspends them starting December 15, 2026 and deletes them starting January 15, 2027. Convert them to managed environments or export your solutions first.

Where do we find upcoming Dynamics 365 features now?

On Microsoft’s AI at Work roadmap. Microsoft is retiring Release Planner by November 15, 2026 and archiving its release plans on Microsoft Learn.
