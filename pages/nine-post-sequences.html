Creating and Managing Sequences in Dynamics 365 Sales

Admin and how-to

By Kristin CanigliaJanuary 5, 2022Updated November 10, 20265 min read

The short answerA sequence is a set of sales activities, such as emails, calls and tasks, that sellers follow in order. In Dynamics 365 Sales, you build sequences in the sales accelerator. Go to Sales Insights settings, select Sequences, then New sequence. Add your steps, save and activate. Then connect the sequence to leads or opportunities, by hand or with Power Automate.

Update, November 2026

What changed since this post was written?

This post is from January 2022. Every step below was checked against Microsoft Learn on 10 November 2026 and rewritten where the product has changed.

  • Microsoft now documents sequences as part of the sales accelerator in Dynamics 365 Sales. The settings area is still called Sales Insights settings.
  • A Sales Enterprise license includes the sales accelerator with 1,500 sequence-connected records a month. Sales Premium has no limit (Microsoft Learn, checked 10 November 2026).
  • New sequences can start from a template. Steps now include conditions, commands and LinkedIn activities.
  • You can connect a sequence four ways, and connect more than one sequence to a record.
  • You can edit an active sequence without deactivating it. Doing so creates a new version.

Sequences give sellers a set of steps that reflect the company’s best practices. Sales managers and admins create them. They decide which activities sellers perform, and in what order.

That helps sellers prioritize their day, stay productive and follow the business process. When this post was written in 2022, sequences were a feature of Dynamics 365 Sales Insights. Today, Microsoft includes them in the sales accelerator (checked 10 November 2026).

How do you create a new sequence?

You create it in Sales Insights settings, under Sales accelerator. These are the current steps from Microsoft’s guide to creating and activating a sequence (checked 10 November 2026):

  1. Sign in to your sales app. In the lower-left corner, go to Change area and select Sales Insights settings.
  2. Under Sales accelerator, select Sequences.
  3. Select New sequence.
  4. Choose Use template or Use blank sequence.
  5. Enter a sequence name, an optional description and the entity. The entity is lead by default; you can choose opportunity instead.
  6. Select Next. The sequence designer opens with a Sequence start here step.
  7. Select the Add icon (+) to add each step. Step types are:
    • Steps: seller tasks such as an email, a phone call or a task
    • Conditions: the next action depends on whether a condition is met
    • Commands: the next action depends on a field value or a process stage
    • LinkedIn: social selling activities
  8. Space out steps with Set wait time. Do not end the sequence with a wait time, or activation can fail.
  9. Save the sequence.
  10. Select Activate, then Activate again to confirm.

Activating is the important last step. Only active sequences can be connected to records. An organization can have up to 250 active sequences at a time (checked 10 November 2026).

How do you connect a sequence to a record?

You connect it by hand or automatically. Once connected, the sequence’s activities show in order under Up next on the record. Microsoft’s page on connecting a sequence to records lists four ways (checked 10 November 2026). You can connect through the sequence, the record grid view, a single record, or Power Automate.

How do you connect a sequence to a record manually?

Start from the list of leads or opportunities:

  1. Go to Change area and select Sales.
  2. Select Leads or Opportunities.
  3. Select one or more records.
  4. Select Connect sequence on the command bar.
  5. Choose a sequence and select Connect.

You can also open a single record and select Connect sequence. Or open the sequence, go to its Connected leads tab and select Connect leads. A record can now have more than one sequence connected, for example one for the seller and one for the account manager.

How do you connect a sequence to a record automatically?

Use a Power Automate flow. The flow connects records to the sequence when its trigger meets the flow’s condition. In the flow, add the Microsoft Dataverse action Perform an unbound action and choose msdyn_ConnectSequence. Microsoft lists the fields to fill in under connecting a sequence through Power Automate (checked 10 November 2026).

How do you disconnect a sequence from a record?

Open the sequence’s connected records and select Disconnect. Microsoft’s training tutorial gives these steps (checked 10 November 2026):

  1. In Sales Insights settings, under Sales accelerator, select Sequences.
  2. Select More options next to the sequence, then View sequence.
  3. Select the Connected leads tab (or connected opportunities).
  4. Select the records to disconnect and select Disconnect.
  5. Confirm with Disconnect.

You can also open a connected record and select Disconnect sequence on the toolbar. Once disconnected, sellers no longer see that sequence’s steps on the record. In 2022 this was done from the sequence’s Properties. Today Properties shows the name, description, record type and owner (Microsoft Learn, checked 10 November 2026).

How do you edit a sales sequence?

Open the sequence and either deactivate it, copy it, or select Edit sequence. You need permission to edit sequences. By default, the System Administrator, Sequence manager, Sales Manager and Salesperson roles have it (Microsoft Learn, checked 10 November 2026).

In 2022 you had to deactivate a sequence before editing it. Microsoft’s guide to cloning and editing a sequence now gives three choices (checked 10 November 2026):

  • Deactivate it, edit, then activate it again.
  • Create a copy, edit the copy, then activate it.
  • Edit sequence on an active sequence. This creates a new version. Records already connected stay on the old version (checked 10 November 2026).

To add a step, use the Add icon as you did when creating it. To change a step, select it, edit the details in the right pane and select Save. To remove a sequence for good, see Microsoft’s steps to delete a sequence (checked 10 November 2026).

Is it easy to create and manage sequences?

Yes. Once the sales accelerator is set up, creating and managing sequences is straightforward. Sequences help sellers prioritize activities, stay productive and follow your business process.

How do you get started with sequences in Dynamics 365 Sales?

Check your license, then set up the sales accelerator. A Sales Enterprise license includes 1,500 sequence-connected records a month. Sales Premium removes that limit (checked 10 November 2026).

Frequently asked questions

What is a sequence in Dynamics 365 Sales?

A set of sales activities, such as emails, calls and tasks, that sellers follow in order. Managers build sequences in the sales accelerator and connect them to leads or opportunities.

Do I need Sales Premium to use sequences?

No. A Sales Enterprise license includes the sales accelerator with 1,500 sequence-connected records a month. Sales Premium has no monthly limit.

Can I edit a sequence that is already active?

Yes. Select Edit sequence to create a new version. Records already connected stay on the old version. You can also deactivate the sequence or edit a copy.
