How To Track the Last Activity Date in Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales

The short answerMicrosoft Dynamics 365 does not include a built-in last activity date. Add two rollup columns to the lead table: Last Activity Date, the maximum Actual End date of its activities, and Next Activity Due Date, the minimum Due Date of its open activities. Then add both to your forms and views so reps see who needs follow-up.

The steps below show how, and the full tutorial is also on YouTube.

Watch the full tutorial on YouTube: Easily Track Last & Next Activity Dates in Dynamics 365

Why Tracking Activity Dates Matters

Knowing the last activity date allows sales teams to quickly see when they last engaged with a lead, contact, or account. If a prospect hasn’t been contacted in a while, it might be time to re-engage.

Meanwhile, the next activity due date ensures that sales reps never miss an upcoming call, meeting, or email. This field provides a clear schedule of future interactions, improving responsiveness and boosting conversion rates.

How to Create Last and Next Activity Date Fields in Dynamics 365

To automate activity tracking in Dynamics 365, follow these steps:

Step 1: Create the Last Activity Date Field

  • Go to Power Apps (make.powerapps.com) and open the Lead table.
  • Create a new column called Last Activity Date.
  • Set the data type to Date and Time, format it as Date Only, and change its behavior to a Rollup field.
  • Configure the rollup settings: select Activities as the related entity, and set the aggregation to Maximum (Max) of the Actual End Date of the activity.
  • Save and close the configuration.

Step 2: Create the Next Activity Due Field

  • Repeat the above steps, naming the field Next Activity Due Date.
  • Set the aggregation to Minimum (Min) of the Due Date instead of Max.
  • Filter the Activity Records to use only Open Activities.
  • Save the field and add it to the form.

Adding the Fields to Views and Forms

Once the fields are created, add them to your Lead forms for easy access. You should also include them in your Open Leads views to monitor past and future activities at a glance.

Improve CRM Tracking with Rollup Fields

By adding these rollup fields, your team can stay organized, follow up on time, and never miss a critical opportunity.

Note: Existing records fill in about 12 hours after you create the column. After that, values refresh about every hour, or right away with Recalculate on the form. See rollup columns on Microsoft Learn (checked 10 November 2026).
