What did RLH need?

RLH (Riordan, Lewis, and Haden) is a private equity firm headquartered in Los Angeles, with a history of over four decades. It invests across business services, healthcare, and government services.

RLH needed to maintain strong client communication and efficient information flow to a global client base.

What did we change?

We migrated RLH's on-premises CRM system to cloud-based Dynamics 365.

We customized the Dynamics 365 mobile app to deliver key information on the go, supporting client interactions and meetings.

What changed day to day?

End-user engagement has risen significantly. Staff have instant access to vital data, management has better insight into employee activities, and meeting notes and follow-up information are faster to create and find.
