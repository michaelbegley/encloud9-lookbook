What was wrong with Hogan’s CRM?

Hogan’s Dynamics 365 system had been customized so heavily that it had become “virtually unusable.” Sales reps avoided it, and management lacked the pipeline visibility it needed to drive performance.

Hogan found enCloud9 through one of our YouTube videos.

What did we change?

We removed the unnecessary customizations and moved Hogan to the out-of-the-box Dynamics 365 Sales Hub, with a standard lead-to-opportunity process.

We also trained the sales team and configured their mailboxes.

What changed day to day?

The sales team now works in a system that is easy to use, with consistent workflows. Activities and follow-ups are trackable, and management can see the pipeline.

What comes next for Hogan?

Hogan plans to add sequences, the Dynamics 365 Sales feature that lines up a set of steps (emails, calls and tasks) for sellers to follow, to automate more of its sales process.
