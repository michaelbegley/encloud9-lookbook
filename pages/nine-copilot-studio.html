Copilot Studio agents

Copilot Studio is Microsoft's tool for building your own AI agents: agents that answer questions from your data and take actions, such as creating a case or updating a record. It runs on the same data as Dynamics 365. Microsoft prices it by Copilot Credits, sold in packs or pay as you go.

When does a business need its own Copilot Studio agent?

When the agents built into Dynamics 365 do not cover the job: questions only your own documents can answer, or a task that crosses your systems. Start with the built-in agents, then build your own for what is left.

What is Copilot Studio?

Microsoft's tool for building your own AI agents. An agent answers questions from your data and can take actions, such as creating a case or updating a record.

How is Copilot Studio different from Copilot in Dynamics 365?

Copilot in Dynamics 365 Sales and Customer Service comes built in. Copilot Studio is for agents you design for your own business. See Copilot and AI.

How much does Copilot Studio cost?

Microsoft sells Copilot Credits: a pack of 25,000 credits is $200 a month for your whole company, or you can commit up front or pay as you go. Our build work is quoted in writing after a short call.

What uses Copilot Credits?

Each answer or action an agent completes uses a varying number of credits. Agents published to Microsoft 365 Copilot are included for users licensed for Microsoft 365 Copilot.

Can an agent work with our Dynamics 365 data?

Yes. Copilot Studio runs on the same platform as Dynamics 365, so an agent can read and update your records within the security roles you set.
