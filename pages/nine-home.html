How much does Dynamics 365 cost?

Microsoft list prices for Dynamics 365 Sales are $65, $105 or $150 per user per month, paid yearly. Setup is quoted in writing after a short call, and support starts at $500 a month. See our pricing guide.

How long does a Dynamics 365 setup take?

Our accelerators take [Brian sets] weeks. On our 9 published projects with a delivery time, go-live took 1 to 6 months, and 7 of the 9 took three months or less. You get the plan, with dates, before we build.

Can you take over a system another partner built?

Yes. Rescue and Repair is for Dynamics 365 systems that are over-customized, stalled or out of date. We review it first and tell you what to keep.

Is Copilot included with Dynamics 365?

Copilot is built into Dynamics 365 Sales Enterprise and Premium. Agents run on Copilot Credits, which most plans buy separately. We tell you what it costs to run before anything is switched on.

What is a generative page?

A page in your Dynamics 365 app that is built from a plain-words request, such as a report on deals that have gone quiet. It runs inside your Dynamics 365 app, on your live records.
