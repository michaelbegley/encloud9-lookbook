How to Improve Dynamics 365 Data Quality: Garbage In, Garbage Out

The short answerPoor Dynamics 365 data quality builds up over time, not overnight. Duplicates, skipped fields, outdated processes and data nobody owns all add up. Fix it at the source. Simplify data entry, give the data an owner and automate how it is captured. Check for duplicates as routine, and show users why the data matters.

The phrase garbage in, garbage out has been around for decades. It still explains why CRM projects succeed or fail.

Dynamics 365 Sales can give you useful insight into customers, pipeline and performance. If the data going in is incomplete, stale, duplicated or wrong, the reports, forecasts and AI recommendations are unreliable too.

enCloud9 has worked with Dynamics 365 for years and has seen this firsthand. When reports look wrong or users stop trusting the system, companies often blame the CRM. The real problem is usually data quality.

Better data quality does not mean starting over. It means better processes, habits and governance around what goes into the system.

Why do Dynamics 365 data quality problems happen?

They happen slowly, over years. Data quality rarely fails in one moment. It slips through:

  • Duplicate records
  • Fields that people skip
  • Setups that no longer match how the business works
  • Information that nobody owns

Eventually, people stop trusting the system. Cleanup projects help for a while, but the same problems often return months later. That is because the cause is a gap in process and adoption, not a handful of isolated errors.

How does simpler data entry improve data quality?

Simpler entry makes accurate records more likely. Complexity is one of the biggest causes of poor data quality. More required fields, more screens and more steps push users toward shortcuts.

Review your system with these questions:

  • Are all required fields truly necessary?
  • Are your forms laid out for fast, accurate data entry?
  • Are users asked to enter information they don’t actually use?
  • Are there outdated fields that should be removed?
  • Does the CRM reflect how your team works today?

When entry is simple, records stay accurate and current. Over time, that builds Dynamics 365 data quality.

Who should own the data in Dynamics 365?

Specific people or teams should own it. Without that, nobody does. Sales assumes marketing owns customer data. Marketing assumes sales does. Leadership assumes everyone keeps the CRM current.

Organizations with strong data quality name the people or teams who keep accounts, contacts, opportunities and customer information accurate. When ownership is clear, accountability follows.

How does automation improve Dynamics 365 data quality?

Automation captures information the same way every time, so fewer mistakes get in. Manual processes leave room for error.

Dynamics 365 Sales has several options that help:

  • Business rules
  • Power Automate workflows
  • Duplicate detection
  • Integrations with other systems

enCloud9 has seen automation deliver bigger long-term gains than large cleanup projects. The aim is to stop bad data getting in, not just to clean it up afterward.

How do you manage duplicate records before they damage data quality?

Make duplicate detection and review a routine part of CRM administration, not an occasional cleanup. Duplicate contacts and accounts confuse users, distort reports and make the system harder to trust.

Data is far easier to maintain when you are dealing with dozens of duplicates instead of thousands. Microsoft explains how to set up duplicate detection rules on Microsoft Learn (checked 10 November 2026).

How are user adoption and data quality connected?

Users keep data current when they understand why it matters. Many organizations enforce data rules without ever explaining them.

Show people how their data feeds:

  • Forecasting
  • Territory planning
  • Pipeline visibility
  • Customer service
  • Executive reporting

Improving adoption and improving data quality are often the same project.

Why does data quality matter more in the age of AI?

AI tools like Microsoft Copilot cannot fix poor CRM data. They tend to expose data problems that went unnoticed before.

Inaccurate input produces inaccurate insight. Organizations with strong data are better placed to benefit from AI.

How do you build long-term Dynamics 365 data quality?

Treat it as an ongoing commitment, not a one-time project. The most successful CRM systems are not always the most customized. They are the ones where people trust the data.

That trust comes from five things:

  • Simple processes
  • Clear ownership
  • Smart automation
  • Regular maintenance
  • Strong adoption

Frequently asked questions

It builds up slowly. Duplicates, skipped fields, setups that no longer match the business and data nobody owns all add up until people stop trusting the system.

Is a one-time data cleanup enough?

Usually not. The same problems often return months later, because the cause is process and adoption. Simpler entry, clear ownership and automation stop bad data getting in.

Does data quality affect Copilot?
