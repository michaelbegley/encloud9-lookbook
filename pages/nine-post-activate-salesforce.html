How Dynamics 365 Activate Makes Leaving Salesforce Easier

Most companies don’t wake up one morning and decide to replace their CRM. In fact, many stay with a system long after they’ve started questioning it.

Maybe costs keep climbing. Maybe users are frustrated. Maybe reporting has become difficult to maintain. Maybe you’re already invested in Microsoft 365 and wonder why your CRM feels disconnected from the rest of your technology stack. But then reality sets in. You have years of customer data. Custom fields. Workflows. Integrations. Reports. User training.

Moving to a new CRM feels a lot like moving houses. You might love the idea of a better home, but the thought of packing everything up and starting over is enough to make you stay where you are. That’s why many organizations continue renewing platforms they aren’t completely happy with. The question is rarely whether another CRM could be better. The question is whether changing is worth the disruption.

The Real Reason Companies Don’t Switch CRMs

When people talk about Salesforce versus Dynamics 365, the conversation often centers on features. In reality, that’s usually not what stops companies from making a move. The biggest obstacle is friction.

Organizations worry about what happens to years of customer history. They wonder how much downtime they’ll face, what migration will cost, and whether they’ll end up with the same problems on a different platform.

We’ve had countless conversations with companies that were interested in Dynamics 365 but hesitant to start the process because the migration itself felt overwhelming. That’s understandable.

A CRM is one of the most important systems in your business. It’s where your sales team works, where your customer information lives, and where many of your critical processes take place. The thought of moving everything somewhere new can feel risky.

Microsoft Is Removing Some of That Friction with Dynamics 365 Activate

Microsoft recently introduced Dynamics 365 Activate, a new AI-powered migration and implementation experience currently in public preview for Salesforce environments. Microsoft says the tool can analyze a Salesforce environment, identify data, business processes, customizations, and dependencies, and provide recommendations that help organizations understand the scope of a migration before they begin.

Dynamics 365 Activate helps organizations get a clearer picture of what’s actually inside their CRM before they start planning a move. That’s important because many businesses have spent years building and modifying their Salesforce environment. Over time, complexity accumulates.

  • Fields nobody uses anymore.
  • Old workflows that still run in the background.
  • Reports created for employees who left years ago.
  • Customizations layered on top of customizations.

Many organizations don’t fully understand how much has accumulated until they begin evaluating a migration.

Dynamics 365 Activate is designed to help uncover those details earlier in the process, making it easier to understand what you’re working with before making major decisions.

A Better CRM, Not Just a New CRM

What’s interesting about Dynamics 365 Activate isn’t the technology itself. It’s what the technology makes possible. For years, a significant portion of a migration project involved figuring out what existed in the current system. If Activate can reduce some of that effort, organizations can spend less time documenting their old CRM and more time planning their future one.

That’s where the real value is. Because the goal shouldn’t be to recreate Salesforce inside Dynamics 365. The goal should be to improve what wasn’t working in the first place.

A migration is one of the few times a business has permission to step back and ask important questions:

  • Do we still need this process?
  • Is this customization adding value?
  • Can we simplify the way our team works?
  • Is our data organized in a way that supports growth?
  • Are we making life easier for our users?

Those are the questions that determine whether a CRM project succeeds. And those questions can’t be answered by software alone. The companies that get the most value from a CRM migration aren’t the ones that move the fastest. They’re the ones that use the opportunity to simplify, modernize, and align their CRM with how the business operates today.

Why More Businesses Are Evaluating Dynamics 365

We’re seeing more companies take a fresh look at Dynamics 365 because they want a CRM that’s more closely aligned with the technology they already use every day. For organizations running Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, Power Platform, and Microsoft Copilot, Dynamics 365 feels like a natural extension of the Microsoft ecosystem. Instead of maintaining separate platforms from different vendors, businesses are looking for ways to simplify operations, improve collaboration, and create a more connected user experience.

That doesn’t automatically make Dynamics 365 the right choice for everyone. But it does make the evaluation worth considering, especially when tools like Dynamics 365 Activate are helping reduce some of the barriers traditionally associated with switching platforms.
