When does a Dynamics 365 team need Power Platform?

When the Dynamics 365 app does most of the job and one piece is missing: a screen, a handoff, a report or a portal. We build that piece on the same data, so nothing is copied between systems.

What is Microsoft Power Platform?

Microsoft's low-code family: Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI, Power Pages and Copilot Studio. It runs on Dataverse, the same data platform as Dynamics 365.

Do we need Power Platform if we have Dynamics 365?

Dynamics 365 already runs on Power Platform. You add Power Platform tools when you need an app, a workflow, a report or a portal that the Dynamics 365 app does not give you.

How much does Power Platform cost?

Microsoft prices each tool separately. Power Apps Premium is $20 per user per month, Power Automate Premium is $15, and Power BI Pro is $14, all paid yearly. Our build work is quoted in writing after a short call.

Which tool should we start with?

Start with the problem. A form or screen your team needs is Power Apps. A manual handoff is Power Automate. A report people build by hand in a spreadsheet is Power BI. A portal for people outside your company is Power Pages.

Can you build a portal for our clients?

Yes. Power Pages portals sit on the same data as Dynamics 365. See the Collaborative for Children story.

What about Copilot and agents?

Copilot Studio builds agents on the same data. See Copilot and AI.
