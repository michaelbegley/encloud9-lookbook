Questions we get asked

  • How much does Dynamics 365 cost?

    Microsoft list prices start at $50 to $65 per user per month and go to $150 to $195 for premium plans, paid yearly. Setup is a fixed package price, and support starts at $500 a month. See our 2026 pricing guide.

  • How long does a Dynamics 365 setup take?

    Our accelerators take [N] weeks. A full implementation typically takes [N to N] months. You get the plan, with dates, before we build.

  • Can you take over a system another partner built?

    Yes. Rescue and Repair is for Dynamics 365 systems that are over-customized, stalled or out of date. We review it first and tell you what to keep.

  • Is Copilot included with Dynamics 365?

    Copilot is built into Dynamics 365 Sales Enterprise and Premium. Agents run on Copilot Credits, which most plans buy separately. We tell you what it costs to run before anything is switched on.
