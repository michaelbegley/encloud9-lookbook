Is Dynamics 365 Sales right for your business?

Dynamics 365 Sales fits a business that sells to other businesses, runs on Microsoft 365, and has outgrown spreadsheets or an old CRM. It is a weaker fit for a very small team that only needs a contact list, or for a business whose main need is accounting and inventory.

A good fit if:

  • Your team already works in Outlook and Teams every day.
  • Leads, quotes and follow-ups live in spreadsheets, inboxes or an old CRM nobody trusts.
  • You want one pipeline view and a forecast your managers believe.
  • You sell through a sales process with stages, quotes or products.
  • You have a Dynamics 365 Sales system that is over-customized or stalled, and want it back to standard.

Probably not a fit if:

  • You have one or two people who only need a shared address book.
  • You want every screen custom-built from scratch. We set up standard features first, because over-customized systems are the ones we most often get called in to rescue.
  • Your main need is accounting, orders and inventory rather than selling. Talk to us and we will tell you honestly where to start.

Not sure? Book a call and we will tell you in 30 minutes.

How do Copilot and AI agents work in Dynamics 365 Sales?

AI in Dynamics 365 Sales comes in three forms: Copilot inside the app, agents Microsoft includes with Dynamics 365 Sales, and agents built for your business in Copilot Studio. Copilot is included in Sales Enterprise and Premium, while agents run on Copilot Credits, so we check your data and permissions and estimate the monthly cost before anything is switched on.

1

Copilot in the app

Copilot in Dynamics 365 Sales summarizes leads, opportunities and accounts, shows what changed on your records, prepares you for meetings, drafts and summarizes emails, answers questions from your SharePoint documents, and brings you news about your accounts. It only sees records the signed-in person can already open.

2

Agents Microsoft includes

Dynamics 365 Sales includes agents that work on their own: researching leads, watching deals for risk and keeping records up to date. We switch on the ones that fit, test them on a small group first, and set a monthly limit on what they can spend.

3

Agents you build

For a job only your business has, we build an agent in Copilot Studio that works on your Dynamics 365 data in Dataverse and can be published to Teams or your website. It is quoted on its own, with its running cost written down.

How much does Dynamics 365 Sales cost?

Microsoft's list price is $65, $105 or $150 per user per month, paid yearly, for Sales Professional, Enterprise or Premium. Setup is a separate project: the Sales Accelerator starts at $[Brian sets], and support plans start at $500 a month. Agents can add Copilot Credit costs, which we estimate before you switch them on.

How long does a Dynamics 365 Sales implementation take?

The Sales Accelerator takes [Brian sets] weeks. On our 8 published projects with a delivery time, go-live took 1 to 6 months, and 7 of the 8 took three months or less. The Schiff Group went live on a new Dynamics 365 Sales system in one month.

Do we need Sales Professional, Enterprise or Premium?

Most B2B sales teams start with Sales Enterprise, because it adds Copilot, forecasting and territory management to the core CRM. Professional suits small teams that need core pipeline tracking without Copilot. Premium suits teams that plan to run agents at volume, since it includes 1,000 Copilot Credits per user per month.

Is Copilot included in Dynamics 365 Sales?

Copilot in Dynamics 365 Sales, Outlook and Teams is included in Sales Enterprise and Sales Premium, not in Sales Professional. The Sales agents are available on all three plans but run on Copilot Credits. Only Sales Premium includes credits; on other plans you buy them separately.

What do the Sales agents cost to run?

Agents use Copilot Credits for each answer and action. Microsoft sells credits in packs of 25,000 for $200 a month, or pay-as-you-go through an Azure subscription. We estimate your monthly use with Microsoft's estimator and set a spending limit before an agent goes live.

Can you fix a Dynamics 365 Sales system another partner set up?

Yes. Our Rescue and Repair package takes over systems that are over-customized, stalled or out of date, and takes them back to standard where it can. Hogan Assessments' over-customized system was back to standard within three months, on time and under budget.

Do we need a support plan after go-live?

Yes. New clients start on at least the Stability plan, $500 a month for 4 hours, on a 12-month agreement. Monthly plans include a 1-hour response during business hours, from 7:00am to 5:00pm Central.
