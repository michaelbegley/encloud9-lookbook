When does a manufacturer need Dynamics 365?

When divisions sell differently, leads pile up faster than people can route them, or an old CRM nobody uses still holds the customer history.

Book a call

A good fit if

  • Several divisions with different sales processes. That was REI.
  • Leads from many regions or products need routing to the right rep. That was JASCO.
  • Jobs, estimates or design work are tracked by hand. That was PPC.
  • A Dynamics 365 project stalled with another partner. That was Tension Envelope.

Do you work with manufacturers?

Yes. Our clients include makers of bus safety systems, analytical instruments, flexible packaging, envelopes, packaging equipment and wastewater equipment.

Can Dynamics 365 handle several divisions?

Yes. For REI we built sales processes for five divisions in one Dynamics 365 system, with dashboards that show management the sales trends.

Can you connect Dynamics 365 to our other systems?

Yes. For Tension Envelope we connected Dynamics 365 to its data warehouse, and for JWC Environmental we connected web leads to Dynamics 365 with automatic assignment.

What about our ERP?

Dynamics 365 Sales and Customer Service handle the customer side. For connecting them to your ERP, tell us what you run and we will tell you what the link takes.

What does it cost?

Our build work is a fixed price, quoted in writing after a short call. Microsoft licenses are a separate cost, and support plans start at $500 a month.
