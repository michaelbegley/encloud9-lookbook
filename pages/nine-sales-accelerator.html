Is the Sales Accelerator right for your team?

The Sales Accelerator fits a business starting on Dynamics 365 Sales for the first time, with a standard sales process and data in spreadsheets or one old CRM. It is a weaker fit if you need integrations or data from several systems on day one, or if you already have a Dynamics 365 system that is not working.

How does a Sales Accelerator project run?

Every project runs through the same four stages: Plan, Build, Go live and Run. You see the written plan before we build, and every change after that is written down and approved.

Stage APlanWe learn how you sell, using a written discovery questionnaire, and give you the plan: users, sales stages, data to move, integrations, and where Copilot and agents fit.
Stage BBuildWe build Dynamics 365 Sales to the plan, using standard features first, and move your data. You check the imported records against your old system before sign-off.
Stage CGo liveTraining and cut-over day. Copilot is switched on if your plan includes it; agents are scoped as a separate pilot.
Stage DRunYour support plan starts. Health checks and updates are logged, so you always know what changed.

How much does the Sales Accelerator cost?

The Sales Accelerator is a fixed $7,500 for our build work. Microsoft licenses are a separate cost: Microsoft's list price is $65, $105 or $150 per user per month, paid yearly, for Sales Professional, Enterprise or Premium. Support plans start at $500 a month.

How long does the Sales Accelerator take?

The Sales Accelerator takes [Brian sets] weeks. You get the plan, with dates, before we build. The Schiff Group went live on a new Dynamics 365 Sales system in one month.

Are Microsoft licenses included?

No. Microsoft licenses and Copilot Credits are not part of the package price, and you pay for them separately. As part of the build we assign your licenses to each user and set their security roles, because Microsoft does not let a user open the app until both are done.

Is the Sales Accelerator the same as the sales accelerator in Dynamics 365?

No. Microsoft's sales accelerator is a feature inside Dynamics 365 Sales that gives sellers a prioritized work list and sequences of follow-up steps. Our Sales Accelerator is a package: we build Dynamics 365 Sales for your team to a fixed scope.

Do we need Sales Professional, Enterprise or Premium?

Most B2B sales teams start with Sales Enterprise, because it adds Copilot, forecasting and territory management to the core CRM. Professional suits small teams that need core pipeline tracking without Copilot. Premium suits teams that plan to run agents at volume, since it includes 1,000 Copilot Credits per user per month.

Is Copilot included in Dynamics 365 Sales?

Copilot in Dynamics 365 Sales, Outlook and Teams is included in Sales Enterprise and Sales Premium, not in Sales Professional. The Sales agents are available on all three plans but run on Copilot Credits. Only Sales Premium includes credits; on other plans you buy them separately.

Can you move our data from Salesforce, HubSpot or spreadsheets?

Yes. Yes. The Sales Accelerator imports up to [Brian sets] records from one source. Data from several systems is part of a full implementation. Integrations with other systems are quoted separately.

What if we need more than the package covers?

Anything outside the package is a written change order you approve before work starts. If you need integrations or data from several systems at go-live, a full implementation fits better.

Do we need a support plan after go-live?

Yes. New clients start on at least the Stability plan, $500 a month for 4 hours, on a 12-month agreement. Monthly plans include a 1-hour response during business hours, 7:00am to 5:00pm Central, Monday to Friday.
