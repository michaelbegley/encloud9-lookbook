How does Email Verifier work?

It checks each address when a lead or contact is entered, cleans the records you already have in one pass, and writes the result to the Dynamics 365 record: a quality score and, for a failed address, the reason. Your team acts on that before a campaign goes out.

How do I start with Email Verifier?

Book a call or ask for the free start. Your first 100 verifications are free, and our team sets Email Verifier up in your Dynamics 365 environment, so you see results on your own records before you pick a plan.

Does Email Verifier run inside Dynamics 365?

Yes. Email Verifier is built for Dynamics 365 and runs in your environment. It works with Power Apps, so verification fits the way your team already enters leads and contacts.

What is the quality score?

Each address gets a score that ranks its quality, so marketers can put high-value prospects first and hold back addresses that are likely to bounce.

What kinds of bad addresses does it catch?

It shows why an address failed: disposable, role-based, defunct or undeliverable. Your team sees the reason on the record, not just a pass or fail.

How is this different from the built-in email validation in Dynamics 365?

Built-in validation catches basic problems like bad syntax. Email Verifier checks whether an address can actually receive mail, scores it, and cleans whole lists in bulk.

Does it help with ClickDimensions or Customer Insights campaigns?

Yes. Verifying a list before a send cuts bounces in any email platform, including ClickDimensions and Dynamics 365 Customer Insights.

What does Email Verifier cost?

Annual plans are $240 for 12,000 verifications, $1,080 for 60,000, or $1,920 for 120,000. You can add verification packs anytime, and the first 100 verifications are free.
