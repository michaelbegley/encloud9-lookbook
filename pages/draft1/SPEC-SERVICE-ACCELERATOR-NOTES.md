# Service Accelerator page: copy source and deliberate changes (9 Nov 2026)

Source: project doc claude/site-build/PAGE-SERVICE-ACCELERATOR.md (4 Nov 2026, Agent 18), stage 4 copy, modelled on the Sales Accelerator page. Agents cannot read project docs, so key lines and changes are listed here.

## Spec facts (spec 2.3, read 4 Nov 2026)
- Customer Service Professional $50, Enterprise $105, Premium $195 per user per month, paid yearly.
- Copilot in Customer Service: Enterprise and Premium, not Professional.
- Case Management, Customer Intent, Customer Knowledge Management and Quality Evaluation agents run on Copilot Credits; Premium includes "base Copilot Credit capacity" (no amount given); Case Management and Customer Intent agents need a pay-as-you-go plan on an Azure subscription.
- Plan split: Professional cases and knowledge base; Enterprise adds Copilot, unified routing, Teams integration, analytics and KPI reporting, workflow automation; Premium adds chatbots, live chat, voice; "Azure Communication Services pricing is separate and not included".
- Dynamics 365 Contact Center is a separate product.
- No Microsoft feature is called "service accelerator" (so no name-clash FAQ).
- Stories: no published story describes a first Customer Service build. Compana (St. Louis): "Ongoing support for Dynamics 365 Sales and Customer Service, plus Copilot Studio work on a Shopify integration in early testing." PriceMDs: new build replacing Monday CRM, Sales and Customer Service in its products, no timeline (spec open item 4 names it as a second candidate; STORY-FACTS row 7).
