What was wrong with BG Peterson’s systems?

BG Peterson is a long-standing mechanical services company based in Omaha, Nebraska, providing HVAC, plumbing and facility solutions. As the business grew, it needed better visibility into customer interactions and more reliable financial processes.

BG Peterson had Dynamics 365 Sales for CRM and Business Central for financial management, but struggled to get consistent value from either. On the financial side, ongoing issues in Business Central created bottlenecks and confusion. They centered on bank reconciliations and account balances, sales tax calculations and refunds, month-end and year-end close, and reporting inconsistencies.

Instead of improving efficiency, these challenges created extra manual work and made it difficult for the team to trust their data.

What did enCloud9 change?

enCloud9 first implemented a basic Dynamics 365 Sales environment. It centralizes contacts and accounts, tracks activities and communication history, and gives the team a simple, usable CRM foundation. The work included data import and cleanup, so existing customer and contact data was structured correctly and ready for use in Dynamics 365.

The engagement then shifted into ongoing support and optimization, primarily within Business Central. Working closely with the BG Peterson team, enCloud9 resolved bank reconciliation issues and cleaned up and reorganized general ledger accounts. It supported month-end and year-end close and addressed sales tax calculation and reporting challenges. It also created and tested new company environments to isolate issues.

The team also made targeted Business Central customizations, such as salesperson-related adjustments, and helped troubleshoot and stabilize financial reports in Power BI.

What changed day to day?

The engagement was more of a stabilization and support effort than a traditional transformation project. Still, the improvements made a meaningful difference in day-to-day operations. Financial processes and reporting are more reliable, and the team has more confidence in its Business Central data. Less time goes to troubleshooting recurring system issues, and the team has ongoing access to expert support.

Implementing the software was only the first step. Pairing the CRM setup with hands-on Business Central support moved BG Peterson from frustration and uncertainty to a more stable system.
