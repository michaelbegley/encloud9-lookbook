The Schiff Group: Cloud-based sales in one month

The Schiff Group, a Rockville, MD firm that guides contractors through government contracting, lacked a comprehensive sales app to track pipelines and opportunities. We implemented Dynamics 365 Sales Hub for accounts, contacts, opportunities, opportunity products and email, and migrated their data. In one month they moved to a cloud-based system with one central platform for sales.

What did The Schiff Group need?

The Schiff Group guides contractors through securing contracts with federal, state and local governments, and provides strategic consulting to help clients navigate government procurement.

It needed a more effective way to manage customer communications and data. Without a comprehensive sales app, it was limited in tracking critical business information, including sales pipelines and opportunity details.

What did we build?

We implemented Dynamics 365 Sales Hub to manage accounts, contacts, opportunities, opportunity products and email communications.

The work included data migration so all relevant information carried into the new system.

What changed day to day?

Within one month The Schiff Group was working in a cloud-based system, a centralized platform for tracking and managing sales opportunities. The firm was particularly pleased with the Opportunity Product feature, which improved how it tracks and manages sales opportunities.

What comes next for The Schiff Group?

The Schiff Group is continuing its partnership with enCloud9 for ongoing support and is considering additional integrations, such as with SharePoint.
