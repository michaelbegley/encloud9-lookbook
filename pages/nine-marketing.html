Marketing automation questions we get asked

Is Click the same as ClickDimensions?

Yes. ClickDimensions now goes by Click. Its website describes it as marketing automation for Microsoft CRM, built to integrate natively with Dynamics 365, and its address is still clickdimensions.com.

Should we use Click or Dynamics 365 Customer Insights?

Both run marketing on your Dynamics 365 data. Customer Insights is Microsoft's own app, priced per tenant at $1,700 a month paid yearly, or $1,000 with attach for organizations with 10 or more users of a qualifying Dynamics 365 app. Click is a separate product built for Dynamics 365. We compare both against your list size, channels and budget on a call and tell you which fits.

Does Click work with our website forms?

Yes. Click builds its own forms and landing pages, and its Form Capture connects an existing HTML form on your site, with a separate option for Gravity Forms on WordPress, so submissions reach Dynamics 365.

Can Click email an account, not just a contact?

Yes. Click emails can be sent to account records, to the account's own email address. Click forms and web tracking match people as leads or contacts, not accounts.

Do we need a support plan after go-live?

Yes. New clients start on at least the Stability plan, $500 a month for 4 hours, on a 12-month agreement. Monthly plans include a 1-hour response during business hours, 7:00am to 5:00pm Central, Monday to Friday.
