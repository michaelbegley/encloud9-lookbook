Copilot, AI and Power Platform

27 questions

What are generative pages in Dynamics 365?

They are pages in model-driven apps that Microsoft's app agent builds from a plain-language description. The agent writes React code that reads and updates your Dataverse data.From Copilot and AI →

Are generative pages generally available?

Yes. Generative pages in model-driven apps are generally available. Using Power Platform connectors and Dataverse custom APIs with them is still in preview.From Copilot and AI →

Does building generative pages cost extra?

Microsoft says the maker experience doesn't require any additional AI or message credits. Users still need a license for the app the page sits in.From Copilot and AI →

Do generative pages replace Power BI or Copilot?

No. Use Power BI for heavy analytics, Copilot for questions about your data, and generative pages for focused working screens.From Copilot and AI →

When does another tool work better than a generative page?

Simple data entry, big reporting over lots of data, or a chat-style need. We will say so in Plan and point you to the better tool.From Copilot and AI →

What is Copilot Studio?

Microsoft's tool for building your own AI agents. An agent answers questions from your data and can take actions, such as creating a case or updating a record.From Copilot Studio →

How is Copilot Studio different from Copilot in Dynamics 365?

Copilot in Dynamics 365 Sales and Customer Service comes built in. Copilot Studio is for agents you design for your own business. See Copilot and AI.From Copilot Studio →

How much does Copilot Studio cost?

Microsoft sells Copilot Credits: a pack of 25,000 credits is $200 a month for your whole company, or you can commit up front or pay as you go. Our build work is quoted in writing after a short call.From Copilot Studio →

What uses Copilot Credits?

Each answer or action an agent completes uses a varying number of credits. Agents published to Microsoft 365 Copilot are included for users licensed for Microsoft 365 Copilot.From Copilot Studio →

Can an agent work with our Dynamics 365 data?

Yes. Copilot Studio runs on the same platform as Dynamics 365, so an agent can read and update your records within the security roles you set.From Copilot Studio →

What is Microsoft Power Platform?

Microsoft's low-code family: Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI, Power Pages and Copilot Studio. It runs on Dataverse, the same data platform as Dynamics 365.From Power Platform →

Do we need Power Platform if we have Dynamics 365?

Dynamics 365 already runs on Power Platform. You add Power Platform tools when you need an app, a workflow, a report or a portal that the Dynamics 365 app does not give you.From Power Platform →

How much does Power Platform cost?

Microsoft prices each tool separately. Power Apps Premium is $20 per user per month, Power Automate Premium is $15, and Power BI Pro is $14, all paid yearly. Our build work is quoted in writing after a short call.From Power Platform →

Which tool should we start with?

Start with the problem. A form or screen your team needs is Power Apps. A manual handoff is Power Automate. A report people build by hand in a spreadsheet is Power BI. A portal for people outside your company is Power Pages.From Power Platform →

Can you build a portal for our clients?

Yes. Power Pages portals sit on the same data as Dynamics 365. See the Collaborative for Children story.From Power Platform →

What about Copilot and agents?

Copilot Studio builds agents on the same data. See Copilot and AI.From Power Platform →

What is Power Apps?

Microsoft's tool for building business apps with little code. Apps run on Dataverse, the same data platform as Dynamics 365, in a browser or on a phone.From Power Apps →

Is Dynamics 365 built on Power Apps?

Yes. Dynamics 365 Sales and Customer Service are model-driven Power Apps. That is why we can add your own screens and forms without moving data anywhere.From Power Apps →

How much does Power Apps cost?

Power Apps Premium is $20 per user per month, paid yearly, or $12 at 2,000 seats or more. The Developer Plan is free for building and testing. Our build work is quoted in writing after a short call.From Power Apps →

Do our Dynamics 365 users need a separate Power Apps license?

Not always. A Dynamics 365 license covers apps that extend that Dynamics 365 app, in the same environment. An app used outside that context needs Power Apps licensing. We check your licenses before you buy anything new.From Power Apps →

What changes in February 2027?

Microsoft starts enforcing Power Apps licensing more strictly. Users of model-driven apps, apps in managed environments and apps used outside a Dynamics 365 environment need a qualifying license, or they cannot open the app. Check your license assignments before then.From Power Apps →

Power Apps or a generative page?

A generative page builds one focused page from a prompt. A Power App is a full app with forms, screens and logic. See generative pages.From Power Apps →

What is Power BI?

Microsoft's reporting tool. It connects to Dynamics 365 and many other data sources and turns them into reports and dashboards in a browser or on a phone.From Power BI →

Do we need Power BI if Dynamics 365 has dashboards?

Not always. Dynamics 365 dashboards cover day-to-day pipeline and service views; we built them for BSI and REI. Power BI is for reporting across systems or heavier analysis.From Power BI →

How much does Power BI cost?

Power BI Pro is $14 per user per month and Premium Per User is $24, both paid yearly. A free account works for your own reports. Our build work is quoted in writing after a short call.From Power BI →

Who needs a Power BI license?

Anyone who shares reports, and anyone who views a shared report, needs Pro or Premium Per User, unless your company buys capacity.From Power BI →

Does Copilot replace Power BI?

No. Use Power BI for heavy analytics and Copilot for questions about your data. See Copilot and AI.From Power BI →

Both run marketing on your Dynamics 365 data. Does Click work with our website forms?

Yes. Click builds its own forms and landing pages, and its Form Capture connects an existing HTML form on your site, with a separate option for Gravity Forms on WordPress, so submissions reach Dynamics 365.From Marketing →

Can Click email an account, not just a contact?

Yes. Click emails can be sent to account records, to the account's own email address. Click forms and web tracking match people as leads or contacts, not accounts.From Marketing →

How do I start with Email Verifier?

Book a call or ask for the free start. Your first 100 verifications are free, and our team sets Email Verifier up in your Dynamics 365 environment, so you see results on your own records before you pick a plan.From Email Verifier →

Does Email Verifier run inside Dynamics 365?

Yes. Email Verifier is built for Dynamics 365 and runs in your environment. It works with Power Apps, so verification fits the way your team already enters leads and contacts.From Email Verifier →

What is the quality score?

Each address gets a score that ranks its quality, so marketers can put high-value prospects first and hold back addresses that are likely to bounce.From Email Verifier →

What kinds of bad addresses does it catch?

It shows why an address failed: disposable, role-based, defunct or undeliverable. Your team sees the reason on the record, not just a pass or fail.From Email Verifier →

How is this different from the built-in email validation in Dynamics 365?

Built-in validation catches basic problems like bad syntax. Email Verifier checks whether an address can actually receive mail, scores it, and cleans whole lists in bulk.From Email Verifier →

Does it help with ClickDimensions or Customer Insights campaigns?

Yes. Verifying a list before a send cuts bounces in any email platform, including ClickDimensions and Dynamics 365 Customer Insights.From Email Verifier →

What does Email Verifier cost?

Annual plans are $240 for 12,000 verifications, $1,080 for 60,000, or $1,920 for 120,000. You can add verification packs anytime, and the first 100 verifications are free.From Email Verifier →
