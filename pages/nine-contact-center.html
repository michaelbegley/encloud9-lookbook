When does a service team need Contact Center?

When customers reach you by phone and chat, and your reps work cases in Dynamics 365 Customer Service. Contact Center routes those conversations into the same system, so nothing is retyped.

What is Dynamics 365 Contact Center?

Microsoft's contact center app. It routes phone, chat and messaging conversations to the right rep and works with Dynamics 365 Customer Service, with Copilot help for reps.

How much does Contact Center cost?

Contact Center is $110 per user per month for voice and digital channels, or $95 for Digital or Voice alone, paid yearly. Phone calls are billed separately through Azure Communication Services.

Do we need Customer Service too?

Contact Center handles the conversations; Customer Service handles cases, queues and knowledge. Customer Service Premium covers both in one plan at $195 per user per month.

What does Copilot do in Contact Center?

Copilot writes conversation summaries and reply drafts and answers from your knowledge articles. AI self-service agents can handle simple requests before a rep picks up.

What does setup cost?

Our build work is a fixed price, quoted in writing after a short call. Microsoft licenses are a separate cost, and support plans start at $500 a month.
