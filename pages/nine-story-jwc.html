What did JWC need?

JWC Environmental has made municipal wastewater equipment since 1973, offering waste reduction and screening solutions.

Leads arrived through its website every day, often in the dozens. JWC had already integrated ClickDimensions Marketing Automation, but it needed a more sophisticated lead management system.

What did we build?

We worked closely with JWC Environmental's web development team to connect web-generated leads to Microsoft Dynamics 365.

Leads are assigned automatically based on factors such as customer interests and geography.

What changed day to day?

JWC saves substantial time managing leads and contacts so the team can focus on high-value work. Automated lead distribution and assignment made the team more responsive to new business opportunities.
