What was wrong with Miles Christi's CRM?

Miles Christi is a religious order of priests and brothers, a relatively small non-profit with around 50 members. It was originally established in Michigan and later expanded to California in October 2008.

It used Dynamics CRM 4.0, a platform that could no longer meet its evolving needs. It uses its CRM to manage donations, coordinate volunteer opportunities, organize events and track interactions with interested parties and benefactors, and it needed a more scalable and modern, cloud-based Dynamics 365 environment.

What did we change?

The work began through a referral from a trusted partner. We rebuilt the existing customizations in the Dynamics 365 Unified Interface and migrated historical records dating back to 2000 using Scribe integration tools.

What changed day to day?

The CRM is accessible from anywhere, at any time. Improved workflows and real-time data access increased operational efficiency, so the order can focus more resources on its mission.
