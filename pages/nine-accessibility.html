Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use encloud9.com, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard, zoom or other assistive technology.

What standard do we work to?

We build this site to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) [2.1 level AA, or 2.2 level AA if Brian chooses it].

We test new pages before they go live and again when they change.

What do we check?

  • Automated accessibility tests on every page at phone and desktop widths.
  • Keyboard use: every link, button and menu can be reached and used without a mouse, and focus is visible.
  • Reduced motion: if your device asks for less motion, our animations stop.
  • Text and color contrast, headings in order, and labels on every form field.
  • Pages that work at 200% zoom without sideways scrolling.

Where might you still have trouble?

  • Some parts of the site come from other companies, such as our forms, the booking page and [the chat assistant]. We choose them with accessibility in mind, but we do not control all of their code.
  • Older blog posts and PDF guides may not meet the standard yet. We fix them as we update them.
  • [Known gaps found in testing, listed here with a date.]
