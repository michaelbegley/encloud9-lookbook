Licensing in one minute

How does Dynamics 365 licensing work?

Each full user gets one base license for the highest-priced app they need, plus a lower-priced attach license for each extra app. People who only read records or enter time can use the lower-cost Team Members license instead.

1 · one per full userBase 2 · second appAttach 2 · third appAttach No second full license 3 · view records, enter timeTeam Members
01Base licenseOne per full user, for the highest-priced app that person needs.
02Attach licenseA lower price for a second or third app on the same user, instead of a second full license.
03Team MembersA lower-cost license for people who view records, enter time or update their own items through set apps.

How much does Dynamics 365 cost?

Microsoft list prices start at $50 to $65 per user per month and go to $150 to $195 for premium plans, paid yearly. Accelerators are $7,500, a full implementation starts at $20,000, and Rescue and Repair runs $500 to $10,000, each confirmed in writing. Support starts at $500 a month. See our Dynamics 365 pricing guide.

Is Copilot included with Dynamics 365?

Copilot is built into Dynamics 365 Sales Enterprise and Premium, and into Customer Service Enterprise and Premium. Agents run on Copilot Credits, which most plans buy separately. We tell you what it costs to run before anything is switched on.

Can one person use two Dynamics 365 apps?

Yes. Each full user gets one base license for the highest-priced app they need, plus a lower-priced attach license for each extra app. People who only read records or enter time can use the lower-cost Team Members license.

Can you take over a system another partner built?

Yes. Rescue and Repair is for Dynamics 365 systems that are over-customized, stalled or out of date. We review it first and tell you what to keep.

Do we need a support plan after go-live?

Yes. New clients start on at least the Stability plan, $500 a month for 4 hours, on a 12-month agreement. Monthly plans include a 1-hour response during business hours, 7:00am to 5:00pm Central, Monday to Friday.
