How do Copilot and AI agents work in Dynamics 365 Sales?

AI in Dynamics 365 Sales comes in three forms: Copilot inside the app, agents Microsoft includes with Dynamics 365 Sales, and agents built for your business in Copilot Studio. Copilot is included in Sales Enterprise and Premium, while agents run on Copilot Credits, so we check your data and permissions and estimate the monthly cost before anything is switched on.

How much does Dynamics 365 Sales cost?

Microsoft's list price is $65, $105 or $150 per user per month, paid yearly, for Sales Professional, Enterprise or Premium. Setup is a separate project, quoted in writing after a short call, and support plans start at $500 a month. Agents can add Copilot Credit costs, which we estimate before you switch them on.

How long does a Dynamics 365 Sales implementation take?

The Sales Accelerator takes [Brian sets] weeks. On our 9 published projects with a delivery time, go-live took 1 to 6 months, and 7 of the 9 took three months or less. The Schiff Group went live on a new Dynamics 365 Sales system in one month.

Do we need Sales Professional, Enterprise or Premium?

Most B2B sales teams start with Sales Enterprise, because it adds Copilot, forecasting and territory management to the core CRM. Professional suits small teams that need core pipeline tracking without Copilot. Premium suits teams that plan to run agents at volume, since it includes 1,000 Copilot Credits per user per month.

Is Copilot included in Dynamics 365 Sales?

Copilot in Dynamics 365 Sales, Outlook and Teams is included in Sales Enterprise and Sales Premium, not in Sales Professional. The Sales agents are available on all three plans but run on Copilot Credits. Only Sales Premium includes credits; on other plans you buy them separately.

What do the Sales agents cost to run?

Agents use Copilot Credits for each answer and action. Microsoft sells credits in packs of 25,000 for $200 a month, or pay-as-you-go through an Azure subscription. We estimate your monthly use with Microsoft's estimator and set a spending limit before an agent goes live.

Can you fix a Dynamics 365 Sales system another partner built?

Yes. Our Rescue and Repair package takes over systems that are over-customized, stalled or out of date, and takes them back to standard where it can. Tension Envelope's stalled project from a prior partner was back on track within three months.

Do we need a support plan after go-live?

Yes. New clients start on at least the Stability plan, $500 a month for 4 hours, on a 12-month agreement. Monthly plans include a 1-hour response during business hours, from 7:00am to 5:00pm Central.

Can you move our data from Salesforce, HubSpot or spreadsheets?

Yes. Moving data from your old CRM or spreadsheets is part of a full implementation, and the Sales Accelerator includes an import of up to [Brian sets] records. Integrations with other systems are quoted separately.
