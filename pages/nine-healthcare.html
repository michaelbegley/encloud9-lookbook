What does Microsoft cover for health data?

Microsoft offers a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement that covers Dynamics 365, Power Apps, Power Automate and Power BI. It does not make you compliant on its own: your setup, security roles and processes still have to meet HIPAA.

Is Dynamics 365 HIPAA compliant?

Microsoft includes Dynamics 365 in its HIPAA Business Associate Agreement. Using it does not make you compliant on its own; your setup, access rules and processes have to meet HIPAA too.

Do we need to sign a BAA with Microsoft?

Microsoft makes its HIPAA Business Associate Agreement available by default through its Data Protection Addendum to customers who are covered entities or business associates.
