When does a Dynamics 365 team need Power Apps?

When your team works around the CRM instead of in it: a spreadsheet for one process, a paper form, or a screen built for a different job. We build that piece on your Dynamics 365 data.

What is Power Apps?

Microsoft's tool for building business apps with little code. Apps run on Dataverse, the same data platform as Dynamics 365, in a browser or on a phone.

Is Dynamics 365 built on Power Apps?

Yes. Dynamics 365 Sales and Customer Service are model-driven Power Apps. That is why we can add your own screens and forms without moving data anywhere.

How much does Power Apps cost?

Power Apps Premium is $20 per user per month, paid yearly, or $12 at 2,000 seats or more. The Developer Plan is free for building and testing. Our build work is quoted in writing after a short call.

Do our Dynamics 365 users need a separate Power Apps license?

Not always. A Dynamics 365 license covers apps that extend that Dynamics 365 app, in the same environment. An app used outside that context needs Power Apps licensing. We check your licenses before you buy anything new.

What changes in February 2027?

Microsoft starts enforcing Power Apps licensing more strictly. Users of model-driven apps, apps in managed environments and apps used outside a Dynamics 365 environment need a qualifying license, or they cannot open the app. Check your license assignments before then.

Power Apps or a generative page?

A generative page builds one focused page from a prompt. A Power App is a full app with forms, screens and logic. See generative pages.
