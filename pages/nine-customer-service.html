Is Dynamics 365 Customer Service right for your business?

Dynamics 365 Customer Service fits a business that answers customer requests by email, phone or a portal, runs on Microsoft 365, and has outgrown a shared inbox, spreadsheets or an old help desk tool.

Not sure? Book a call and we will tell you which package fits.

Book a call

A good fit if

  • Your team already works in Outlook and Teams every day.
  • Customer requests live in a shared inbox, spreadsheets or a help desk tool nobody trusts.
  • You want every request tracked as a case, with an owner and a response target.
  • Your customers have support contracts or response times you have to keep.
  • You already use Dynamics 365 Sales and want service on the same customer records.
  • You also need phone and chat routing for a contact center. Ask us about Dynamics 365 Contact Center.

What does Dynamics 365 Customer Service include?

Every Dynamics 365 Customer Service plan covers cases, the knowledge base and service-level agreements, and works with Microsoft 365. Enterprise adds Copilot, unified routing, Teams integration and reporting, and Premium adds chatbots, live chat, a voice channel and base Copilot Credit capacity for the service agents.

Copilot and agents

How do Copilot and AI agents work in Dynamics 365 Customer Service?

AI in Dynamics 365 Customer Service comes in three forms: Copilot inside the app, agents Microsoft includes with Customer Service, and agents built for your business in Copilot Studio. Copilot is included in Customer Service Enterprise and Premium, while agents run on Copilot Credits, so we check your data and permissions and estimate the monthly cost before anything is switched on.

How does a Dynamics 365 Customer Service project run?

Every project runs through the same four stages: Plan, Build, Go live and Run. You see the written plan before we build, and every change after that is written down and approved.

Stage APlanWe learn how you serve your customers, using a written discovery questionnaire, and give you the plan: users, queues, service levels, data to move, integrations, and where Copilot and agents fit.
Stage BBuildWe build Dynamics 365 Customer Service to the plan, using standard features first, and move your data. You check the imported records against your old system before sign-off.
Stage CGo liveTraining, cut-over day, and Copilot switched on. Agents are switched on only where your data, permissions and credit budget are ready.

How much does Dynamics 365 Customer Service cost?

Microsoft's list price is $50, $105 or $195 per user per month, paid yearly, for Customer Service Professional, Enterprise or Premium. Setup is a separate project, quoted in writing after a short call, and support plans start at $500 a month. Agents can add Copilot Credit costs, which we estimate before you switch them on.

How long does a Dynamics 365 Customer Service implementation take?

The Service Accelerator takes [Brian sets] weeks. On our 9 published projects with a delivery time, go-live took 1 to 6 months, and 7 of the 9 took three months or less.

Do we need Customer Service Professional, Enterprise or Premium?

Professional covers cases and the knowledge base, without Copilot. Enterprise adds Copilot in Customer Service, unified routing, Teams integration, analytics and KPI reporting, and workflow automation. Premium adds chatbots, live chat, a voice channel and base Copilot Credit capacity for the service agents.

Is Copilot included in Dynamics 365 Customer Service?

Copilot in Customer Service is included in Customer Service Enterprise and Premium, not in Customer Service Professional. The Case Management, Customer Intent, Customer Knowledge Management and Quality Evaluation agents run on Copilot Credits. Premium includes base Copilot Credit capacity for them; on other plans you buy credits separately. Some agents, including the Case Management and Customer Intent agents, also need a pay-as-you-go plan on an Azure subscription.

Does Customer Service Professional include service-level agreements?

Yes. Microsoft's documentation lists service-level agreements for Customer Service Professional and Enterprise. You cannot mix Professional and Enterprise users in one environment.

Does Dynamics 365 Customer Service include phone and chat?

Live chat and a voice channel come with Customer Service Premium. Azure Communication Services pricing is separate and not included. Dynamics 365 Contact Center is a separate product at $110 per user per month, paid yearly. The Service Accelerator does not include channel setup; we quote that work separately.

Can you fix a Dynamics 365 Customer Service system another partner built?

Yes. Our Rescue and Repair package takes over systems that are over-customized, stalled or out of date, and takes them back to standard where it can.

Do we need a support plan after go-live?

Yes. New clients start on at least the Stability plan, $500 a month for 4 hours, on a 12-month agreement. Monthly plans include a 1-hour response during business hours, 7:00am to 5:00pm Central, Monday to Friday.

Can you move our cases and contacts from another help desk or spreadsheets?

Yes. The Service Accelerator includes an import of up to [Brian sets] records. Moving data from several systems is part of a full implementation. Integrations with other systems are quoted separately.
