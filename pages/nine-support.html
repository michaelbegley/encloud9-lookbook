Is a support plan right for your business?

A support plan fits a business whose Dynamics 365 system is live and needs regular help. If you are still building or your system is not working, start with a package.

How much does Dynamics 365 support cost?

Support starts at $500 a month for 4 hours on the Stability plan, which is the minimum plan for new clients. Optimization is $1,500 a month for 20 hours and Partnership from $2,000 a month for 35 hours. Monthly plans run on a 12-month agreement.

Which support plan fits our team?

Optimization is built for teams of up to 50 users and Partnership for 50 users and up. Stability gives 4 hours a month for teams that need steady break and fix help, enhancements and user management.

What does the 1-hour response mean?

On monthly plans, a person picks up your request within 1 hour during business hours, 7:00am to 5:00pm Central, Monday to Friday. It means the request has been picked up and reviewed, not that it is solved.

Is there a setup or onboarding fee?

New clients pay an onboarding fee of up to one month's charge.

What happens if we need more hours?

Optimization can add blocks of hours, and Partnership gets discounted add-on hours. On-Demand work is $200 an hour.

Do we need a support plan after go-live?

Yes. New clients start on at least the Stability plan, $500 a month for 4 hours, on a 12-month agreement. Monthly plans include a 1-hour response during business hours, 7:00am to 5:00pm Central, Monday to Friday.

Can you support a Dynamics 365 system another partner built?

Yes. If the system needs repair first, Rescue and Repair gets it working, then a support plan keeps it that way.
