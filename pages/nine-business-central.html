Microsoft's built-in integration runs through Dataverse. These are the pieces sales and finance teams use most.

Can Dynamics 365 Sales show Business Central data?

Yes. With Microsoft's integration, sellers see a customer's balance, open invoices and item availability from Business Central while they work in Dynamics 365 Sales.

Do you implement Business Central?

enCloud9 focuses on Dynamics 365 and works with a network of Business Central partners for the accounting setup. enCloud9 connects the two systems so they work as one.

Does Business Central integrate with Customer Service?

Microsoft's built-in integration is with Dynamics 365 Sales only. Other connections can be built with Power Automate or custom work, quoted in writing.

How often does data sync?

Microsoft's default sync jobs run every 30 minutes, and the schedule can be changed. Some updates, like account statistics, can also be run by hand.

What does the integration cost?

Our setup work is quoted in writing after a short call. Business Central and Dynamics 365 are licensed separately, and enCloud9 sells Microsoft licenses.
