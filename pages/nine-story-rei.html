Radio Engineering Industries (REI) in Omaha, NE makes audio, video and safety systems for the school and tour bus industry. What was wrong with REI's CRM?

Radio Engineering Industries, Inc. (REI) is headquartered in Omaha and manufactures audio, video, and safety systems for the school and tour bus industry.

REI's on-premises Dynamics 2011 CRM had grown outdated, which led to low user acceptance. REI wanted to move to Dynamics 365 Online and shift from mostly offline processes to a CRM-centered approach.

What did we change?

We migrated REI to Dynamics 365 Online.

We designed and implemented structured sales processes in Dynamics 365 Sales to fit the needs of REI's five divisions, and built dashboards that give management insight into sales trends.

What changed day to day?

REI's sales work now runs on streamlined, more efficient workflows, with better accessibility across the organization and higher user acceptance and productivity.
