Generative pages

Describe the screen. Watch the page build.

Generative pages let you describe a screen in plain English and get a working page inside a Dynamics 365 app. Microsoft's app agent writes the code and connects it to your Dataverse tables, and you refine it by chatting. The AI builds the page; the finished page is an ordinary screen on your data.

Pick a request
  1. Describe itSay what the screen should show, in plain English.
  2. The agent builds itMicrosoft's app agent writes the code and connects it to your Dataverse tables.
  3. Refine by chattingAsk for a change and the page updates. Test after each one.

Where do generative pages show up?

  • In the app navigation, as its own page.
  • Inside a form, in a section or tab. The page gets the current record automatically, so you keep the standard form for data entry and add a richer view beside it.
  • Opened from another page or button, with the record passed in.

The AI does its work while the page is being built. The finished page is an ordinary screen that reads and updates your Dynamics 365 records. enCloud9 tests it with your users' real security roles before anyone relies on it.

When does another tool work better?

  • The job is simple data entry. A standard form is more consistent and easier to support.
  • You need heavy reporting over a lot of data. Use Power BI.
  • People need to ask questions, not open a screen. Copilot in Dynamics 365 answers questions about your data in a side pane.
  • The need is a conversation or an automated process. Build an agent or workflow in Copilot Studio.
  • You need one control reused across many forms. A PCF code component fits better.
  • Several makers must edit the same page at once. Generative pages do not support co-authoring yet.

Also expect to iterate. A new prompt can change parts of the page you were happy with, so test after each change.

Is Copilot included in Dynamics 365 Sales?

Copilot in Dynamics 365 Sales, Outlook and Teams is included in Sales Enterprise and Sales Premium, not in Sales Professional. The Sales agents are available on all three plans but run on Copilot Credits. Only Sales Premium includes credits; on other plans you buy them separately.

Is Copilot included in Dynamics 365 Customer Service?

Copilot in Customer Service is included in Customer Service Enterprise and Premium, not in Customer Service Professional. The Case Management, Customer Intent, Customer Knowledge Management and Quality Evaluation agents run on Copilot Credits. Premium includes base Copilot Credit capacity for them; on other plans you buy credits separately. Some agents, including the Case Management and Customer Intent agents, also need a pay-as-you-go plan on an Azure subscription.

What do the Sales agents cost to run?

Agents use Copilot Credits for each answer and action. Microsoft sells credits in packs of 25,000 for $200 a month, or pay-as-you-go through an Azure subscription. enCloud9 estimates your monthly use with Microsoft's estimator and sets a spending limit before an agent goes live.

What are generative pages in Dynamics 365?

They are pages in model-driven apps that Microsoft's app agent builds from a plain-language description. The agent writes React code that reads and updates your Dataverse data.

Are generative pages generally available?

Yes. Generative pages in model-driven apps are generally available. Using Power Platform connectors and Dataverse custom APIs with them is still in preview.

Does building generative pages cost extra?

Microsoft says the maker experience doesn't require any additional AI or message credits. Users still need a license for the app the page sits in.

Do generative pages replace Power BI or Copilot?

No. Use Power BI for heavy analytics, Copilot for questions about your data, and generative pages for focused working screens.

When does another tool work better than a generative page?

Simple data entry, big reporting over lots of data, or a chat-style need. enCloud9 will say so before the build and point you to the better tool.
