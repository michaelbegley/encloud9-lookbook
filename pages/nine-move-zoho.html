What moves from Zoho to Dynamics 365?

Your Zoho CRM leads, contacts, accounts, deals and activity history move to Dynamics 365 Sales, mapped field by field and tested in a trial run before the full move. Zoho workflows, blueprints, custom functions and connected apps do not move on their own; we review them first and quote any rebuild separately.

Included

  • Your Zoho modules and fields mapped to Dynamics 365 Sales
  • Your Zoho users mapped to Dynamics 365 users and security roles
  • Your Microsoft licenses assigned to each user
  • A trial run first, then the full move, with record counts checked against Zoho
  • Up to [Brian sets] records moved

Is a move from Zoho right for your team?

A move fits a sales team on Zoho CRM whose setup is mostly standard records, such as leads, contacts, accounts and deals, and who already work in Outlook and Teams. It is a weaker fit if your Zoho CRM account runs many blueprints, custom functions and connected apps, or if you need other systems connected on day one.

How much does a move from Zoho cost?

A move from Zoho is a fixed price for our work, quoted in writing after a short call. Microsoft licenses are a separate cost: Microsoft's list price is $65, $105 or $150 per user per month, paid yearly, for Sales Professional, Enterprise or Premium. Support plans start at $500 a month.

How long does a move from Zoho take?

A move takes [Brian sets] weeks. You get the plan, with dates, before we build.

Do our Zoho workflows and blueprints move across?

Not on their own. Workflows, blueprints, custom functions and connected apps are rebuilt in Dynamics 365 rather than copied. We review them first and quote any rebuild separately.

Does Microsoft have a tool for moving from Zoho CRM?

Not a dedicated one. Microsoft's own migration tool, Dynamics 365 Activate, covers moves from Salesforce. For other systems, Microsoft's guidance points to data import, dataflows and data migration tools such as Azure Data Factory.

Are Microsoft licenses included?

No. Microsoft licenses and Copilot Credits are not part of the package price, and you pay for them separately. As part of the move we assign your licenses to each user and set their security roles, because Microsoft does not let a user open the app until both are done.

Is Copilot included in Dynamics 365 Sales?

Copilot in Dynamics 365 Sales, Outlook and Teams is included in Sales Enterprise and Sales Premium, not in Sales Professional. The Sales agents are available on all three plans but run on Copilot Credits. Only Sales Premium includes credits; on other plans you buy them separately.

Can you move our data from Salesforce, HubSpot or spreadsheets?

Yes. Moving data from your old CRM or spreadsheets is part of a full implementation, and the Sales Accelerator includes an import of up to [Brian sets] records. Integrations with other systems are quoted separately.

What if we need more than the package covers?

Anything outside the package is a written change order you approve before work starts. If you need integrations or data from several systems at go-live, a full implementation fits better.

Do we need a support plan after go-live?

Yes. New clients start on at least the Stability plan, $500 a month for 4 hours, on a 12-month agreement. Monthly plans include a 1-hour response during business hours, 7:00am to 5:00pm Central, Monday to Friday.
