Website look research (3 Nov 2026)

13 research agents, run for the enCloud9 site redo, kept here for any future website. Values are measured (read from live CSS) unless marked inferred. Concept book: https://claude.ai/artifact/H46gc7GBrnWM3Hnrv453kS

The short version

1. Distinct looks come from ONE typographic idea plus colour restraint. Headlines at weight 300 to 590, never 700+, tracked tight (-0.02 to -0.06em). Near-black text, one accent used sparingly. 2. What reads as "AI built": icon-card triplets, all-caps eyebrows, pill badges, invented stat rows, gradients and glows, same fade-up everywhere, default fonts, decorative grid lines, filler slogans. Copying Simulithic's surface is not enough: Geist and grid lines are on that list too. The fix is specificity: show our real thing. 3. Structure: container 1200 to 1440, text columns 520 to 900, one section padding value used everywhere, hairlines instead of shadows. 4. Proof and people: real client stories with names, real photos of the team, a founder face. No partner site we checked puts a human on the hero or shows prices. 5. Build: custom WordPress block theme (theme.json tokens, locked patterns), our own SVG line art, CSS-first motion. Lock the brand in a DESIGN.md file.

Four directions (on the concept book)

1. Ledger: calm precision (DesignMD Tax and Finance + Damir). Instrument Sans 600, navy ink, #355DA6 for actions only, a data panel as hero, tabular numbers, hairline service list. 2. Quiet Product: Simulithic widened to 1200. Geist 400 at -0.05em, two-tone headlines, code-drawn isometric line art of our services, hairline column guides, sky blue only on buttons and art. 3. Editorial: founder-led. Big light Jost (Futura-like, matches the logo) over a full-bleed real photo, small mono labels, Brian's story in his words. 4. Sky Grid: warm and approachable (Enode). Pale sky page, Plus Jakarta Sans 500, outsized lead paragraph, pill nav with a numbered mega menu, rounded photo and number cards.

Open decisions for Brian

- Headline font: Geist + Instrument Sans (agent pick), Plus Jakarta Sans, or keep Lato. Changing it changes the locked brand kit. - Which direction. - Real photos: a half-day shoot (shot list below) vs stock in the meantime.

10-motion.md

MOTION (agent 10): CSS + one small observer script. Scroll-driven CSS: Chrome 115, Safari 26, Firefox flag only (stable 152, Jun 2026) -> enhancement with static fallback. GSAP free incl SplitText since 30 Apr 2025 (only for headline split reveals). Motion inView ~0.5kB. USE: one-time fade-up 8-16px on headings/sections (view() or IntersectionObserver); sticky section headers; hover on transform/opacity 150-250ms in @media(hover:hover); count-up on 1-3 sourced stats only with real number in HTML. AVOID: per-letter blur on body; canvas image-sequence scrollytelling; re-triggering animations; page-builder motion add-ons. Rules: transform/opacity only; reveal once; hidden state only via .js class; wrap in prefers-reduced-motion. WP: enqueue in theme, block classes reveal/count/sticky-head. Examples: Linear Method page fade-ups (per third-party teardown); Chrome case studies (Tokopedia etc).
