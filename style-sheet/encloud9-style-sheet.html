Colours

The nine-step blue scale

Every colour comes from these nine steps. Step 5 is the brand blue, used for main buttons and the one highlight on a page. Steps 1 to 5 take white text. Steps 6 to 9 take dark ink text.

  • One blue per momentUse brand blue (step 5) for the main button and one highlight. Do not spread it across a whole page.
  • Dark sectionsUse step 1 as the background, white text, and step 7 for small labels.
  • Square cornersNo rounded corners on the site. The grid lines are the decoration.
