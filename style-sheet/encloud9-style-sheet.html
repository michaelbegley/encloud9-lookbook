Colors

The nine-step blue scale

Every color comes from these nine steps. Step 5 is the brand blue: the main button and the one highlight on a page. Steps 1 to 5 take white text. Steps 6 to 9 take dark ink text.

White on brand blue6.4 : 1 · pass
White on step 117.1 : 1 · pass
Ink on step 65.3 : 1 · pass
Muted on white7.3 : 1 · pass
White on step 63.3 : 1 · too faint, avoid
  • One blue per momentBrand blue for the main button and one highlight. Do not spread it across a whole page or graphic.
  • Dark sectionsStep 1 or 2 as the background, white text, step 8 for small labels.
  • Square cornersNo rounded corners on the site or graphics. Grid lines are the decoration.
