Is the Service Accelerator right for your team?

The Service Accelerator fits a business starting on Dynamics 365 Customer Service for the first time, with customer requests in a shared inbox, spreadsheets or one old help desk tool. It is a weaker fit if you need integrations, phone and chat channels or data from several systems on day one, or if you already have a Dynamics 365 system that is not working.

How does a Service Accelerator project run?

Every project runs through the same four stages: Plan, Build, Go live and Run. You see the written plan before we build, and every change after that is written down and approved.

Stage APlanWe learn how you serve your customers, using a written discovery questionnaire, and give you the plan: users, queues, service levels, data to move, integrations, and where Copilot and agents fit.
Stage BBuildWe build Dynamics 365 Customer Service to the plan, using standard features first, and move your data. You check the imported records against your old system before sign-off.
Stage CGo liveTraining and cut-over day. Copilot is switched on if your plan includes it; agents are scoped as a separate pilot.
Stage DRunYour support plan starts. Health checks and updates are logged, so you always know what changed.

How much does the Service Accelerator cost?

The Service Accelerator is a fixed $7,500 for our build work. Microsoft licenses are a separate cost: Microsoft's list price is $50, $105 or $195 per user per month, paid yearly, for Customer Service Professional, Enterprise or Premium. Support plans start at $500 a month.

How long does the Service Accelerator take?

The Service Accelerator takes [Brian sets] weeks. You get the plan, with dates, before we build.

Are Microsoft licenses included?

No. Microsoft licenses and Copilot Credits are not part of the package price, and you pay for them separately. As part of the build we assign your licenses to each user and set their security roles, because Microsoft does not let a user open the app until both are done.

Do we need Customer Service Professional, Enterprise or Premium?

Professional covers cases and the knowledge base, without Copilot. Enterprise adds Copilot in Customer Service, unified routing, Teams integration, analytics and KPI reporting, and workflow automation. Premium adds chatbots, live chat, a voice channel and base Copilot Credit capacity for the service agents.

Is Copilot included in Dynamics 365 Customer Service?

Copilot in Customer Service is included in Customer Service Enterprise and Premium, not in Customer Service Professional. The Case Management, Customer Intent, Customer Knowledge Management and Quality Evaluation agents run on Copilot Credits. Premium includes base Copilot Credit capacity for them; on other plans you buy credits separately. Agents also need an Azure subscription; the Case Management and Customer Intent agents bill pay-as-you-go through it.

Can you move our cases and contacts from another help desk or spreadsheets?

Yes. The Service Accelerator includes an import of up to [Brian sets] records. Moving data from several systems is part of a full implementation. Integrations with other systems are quoted separately.

What if we need more than the package covers?

Anything outside the package is a written change order you approve before work starts. If you need integrations or data from several systems at go-live, a full implementation fits better.

Does the Service Accelerator include phone and chat?

No. Voice, live chat and messaging channels are not part of the Service Accelerator. Microsoft includes them in Customer Service Premium or sells them as Dynamics 365 Contact Center, and we quote that work separately. Azure Communication Services pricing is separate and not included.

Do we need a support plan after go-live?

Yes. New clients start on at least the Stability plan, $500 a month for 4 hours, on a 12-month agreement. Monthly plans include a 1-hour response during business hours, 7:00am to 5:00pm Central, Monday to Friday.
