Dynamics 365 licensing

Each full Dynamics 365 user needs one base license for the highest-priced app they use, plus a lower-priced attach license for each extra app. People who only view records or enter time can use the lower-cost Team Members license. Getting this mix right is how you avoid paying twice.

What kinds of Dynamics 365 licenses are there?

Three license types decide most of the bill: base, attach and Team Members. Power Apps use rights, Copilot Credits and storage come with them or on top.

Dynamics 365 license types
LicenseWho it is forWhat to know
Base licenseEvery full userOne per user, for the highest-priced app that person needs
Attach licenseA full user who needs a second appLower price than a second full license
Team MembersPeople who view records, enter time or update their own itemsSet Team Member apps only, customizable with up to 15 extra tables; custom apps need a full license; price from your partner or the Microsoft 365 admin center

How do we know our licenses are right?

Most waste comes from two mistakes: full licenses for people who only need Team Members, and two full licenses where one base plus an attach would do. Book a call and we go through your users, apps and storage with you.

Book a call

Time for a check if

  • Some users only view records or enter time, but have full licenses.
  • One person holds two full licenses for two apps.
  • Your storage is near 100%. Past that, under its new storage checks, Microsoft blocks new environments, and sandboxes lock after 30 and 90 days. See storage.
  • You run custom apps outside a Dynamics 365 environment. Microsoft enforces Power Apps licensing more strictly from February 2027.

What is a Dynamics 365 attach license?

An attach license adds a second app to a user who already has a base license, at a lower price than a full license. The base must be the highest-priced app that user needs.

What can a Team Members user do?

View records, enter time and expenses, and update their own items through Microsoft's Team Member apps, which can be customized with up to 15 extra tables. Team Members does not cover custom apps. If a Team Members user starts doing full work, Microsoft expects a full license.

Do Dynamics 365 users need Power Apps licenses?

Not for apps that extend their licensed Dynamics 365 app in the same environment. Apps used outside that context need Power Apps licensing, and Microsoft enforces this more strictly from February 2027.

What happens if we run out of storage?

Microsoft is rolling out stricter storage checks: past 100%, creating, copying and restoring environments is blocked; after 30 days sandboxes go admin-only, and after 90 days sign-in to them is blocked. Production environments are not locked. Today Microsoft sends email warnings.
