Do you work with professional services firms?

Yes. Our clients include a government contracting consultancy, a business support services firm and a private equity firm, and CRM Software Blog listed us as its Professional Services partner on its 2026 list.

Which Dynamics 365 app does a professional services firm need?

Most start with Dynamics 365 Sales for clients, contacts and pipeline. Firms that run projects, track time and bill for it add Project Operations.

How long does it take?

A full implementation or a Rescue and Repair usually runs 8 to 16 weeks and is quoted for your project. The Schiff Group moved to Dynamics 365 Sales in one month.

Can you fix a CRM we already have?

Yes. Aureon had a Dynamics CRM set up without professional help; we audited it, reviewed its processes and built the plan to fix it. See Rescue and Repair.

What does it cost?

Sales and Service Accelerators are $7,500 fixed, a full implementation starts at $20,000, and Rescue and Repair runs $500 to $10,000, each quoted in writing. Microsoft licenses are a separate cost, and support plans start at $500 a month.
