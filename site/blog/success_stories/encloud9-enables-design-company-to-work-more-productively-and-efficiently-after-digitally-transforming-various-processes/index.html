What did PPC Flex need?

PPC Flexible Packaging, formerly known as Packaging Products, makes flexible packaging in Buffalo Grove, Illinois, including consumer, cleanroom, and floral and gift packaging.

PPC saw that some of its processes had become outdated and needed to digitize and streamline its manual processes to stay competitive. Many clients depend on PPC's design services, so a smooth system for client artwork was essential.

What did we build?

We started with PPC's manual process for managing client artwork.

Using Microsoft Dynamics 365, we built a tracking system that manages client requests, job estimates, and the entire design process.

What changed day to day?

PPC now manages all its jobs in Dynamics 365 through a dashboard, with a calendar that organizes jobs by due date and assigns them to the responsible artists. The system is expected to save hundreds of hours annually.
