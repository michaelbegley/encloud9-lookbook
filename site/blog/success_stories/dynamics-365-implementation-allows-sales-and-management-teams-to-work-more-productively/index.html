BSI: One view from lead to close

Biometric Signature ID (BSI) is a security software company specializing in biometric solutions in Lewisville, TX. It needed an integrated CRM to track and improve its whole sales process, from first lead to closed deal. We implemented Microsoft Dynamics 365 with custom business process flows and real-time dashboards. BSI now has better pipeline management and less manual effort.

What did BSI need?

Biometric Signature ID (BSI) builds software tools for security and serves sectors such as education and finance. It is an emerging startup and a Microsoft partner with over 15 years in biometric security.

BSI needed a way to track and improve its entire sales process, from initial lead generation to closing opportunities, and chose us to implement Microsoft Dynamics 365.

What did we build?

We built customized business process flows in Dynamics 365.

We also built dashboards that give BSI's management team real-time insight into sales activities.

What changed day to day?

Lead management and opportunity tracking are simpler. BSI has better sales pipeline management, less manual effort, and higher overall sales efficiency.
