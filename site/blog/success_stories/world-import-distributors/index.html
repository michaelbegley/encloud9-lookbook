What did WID need?

World Import Distributors is a family-owned business founded in 1991 and based in Mahwah, NJ. It brings cheeses from Europe to the United States. It is known for exclusive products and partnerships with Wisconsin-based dairy farmers.

WID needed to streamline its financial management and complex inventory tracking. It managed its finances and ERP with QuickBooks and a proprietary Access database. Those systems could not manage inventory by lot and other factors, which led to inefficiencies and delays. They began to hinder WID’s growth plans, prompting a search for modern solutions.

What did enCloud9 build?

enCloud9 implemented Business Central, giving WID a single, integrated system for finances, inventory and order management.

enCloud9 worked closely with WID’s main contacts to ensure a smooth transition.

What changed day to day?

The project was completed in 90 days. WID now has a modern accounting system with all data consolidated in one place. It no longer relies on separate systems for inventory and finances, and all data is easily accessible via mobile through the Microsoft cloud.

WID is extremely satisfied with the implementation, particularly the trusted partnership with enCloud9. It now enjoys improved efficiency and streamlined operations.

What comes next for WID?

WID plans to continue working with enCloud9 for future needs.
