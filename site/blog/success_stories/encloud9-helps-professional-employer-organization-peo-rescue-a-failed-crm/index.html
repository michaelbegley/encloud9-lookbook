What was wrong with Aureon's CRM?

Aureon is a business support services firm headquartered in Des Moines, IA. It was founded in 1986 as Iowa Network Services to connect independent telephone companies, and rebranded in 2016.

Several years earlier, Aureon implemented Dynamics CRM without professional guidance. The system struggled to meet its requirements, which led to underuse and reliability issues.

What did we change?

We started with a thorough systems audit and a process review, done together with Aureon.

From that work we built a CRM enhancement plan aimed at Aureon's unique challenges.

What changed day to day?

For the first time in its history, Aureon and its staff can rely on Dynamics 365 for real-time, relevant information, and use it to manage client relationships.
