Milestone Leasing: a cleaner CRM that tracks renewals

Milestone Leasing, a trailer, chassis and container leasing company in St. Charles, Missouri, had a Dynamics 365 system that had become hard to use, with reps updating opportunities by hand. enCloud9 cleaned it up, automated lease renewals, lead routing and quote approvals, and set reps up to create standard quotes directly from the CRM.

What was wrong with Milestone’s CRM?

Milestone Leasing had an existing Dynamics 365 system, but it had become hard to use. Updating opportunities by hand could take each rep significant time, so the CRM felt more like a burden than a help.

What did enCloud9 change?

We removed unused components, cleaned the data and simplified workflows. We streamlined lead and opportunity management, replaced manual renewal tracking with automated renewals, task assignments and follow-ups, and set up lead routing and quote approvals.

Reps now create standard quotes directly from the CRM with Document Core Pack.

What changed day to day?

The CRM now matches how Milestone sells and renews leases: renewals and follow-ups are automated, and the pipeline reflects real sales activity. We continue to work with Milestone on further improvements.
