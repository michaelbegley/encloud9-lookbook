What did Elmwood need?

Elmwood Reclaimed Timber, based in Peculiar, MO, is a top provider of natural wood products for homeowners, homebuilders and architects.

It wanted to migrate its existing on-premises Dynamics 365 system to the Microsoft Cloud. A former consultant coworker of Brian Begley recommended enCloud9.

What did we change?

With help from the Microsoft Fast Track team, we moved the CRM to the cloud. We used Tibco (Scribe) for data transfer and connected Elmwood's on-premises GP ERP for order submissions, along with Dynamics GP and ClickDimensions.

What changed day to day?

Elmwood now uses cloud-specific features such as Sales Assistance for round-robin lead assignments and sequences. The migration reduced its server room needs and streamlined its sales processes.

What comes next for Elmwood?

Elmwood continues to work with enCloud9 to further clean up its data.
