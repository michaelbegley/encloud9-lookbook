What did Compana need?

Compana is a growing organization focused on delivering high-quality pet products across multiple brands. It had already invested in Microsoft Dynamics 365 and needed a partner to help maintain and expand that system.

Compana wanted to improve customer engagement through digital channels, explore AI-driven customer service, keep its CRM and service systems stable as usage scaled, and find practical ways to extend Dynamics 365 into new areas, including eCommerce.

What did we change?

We partnered with Compana under a continuous support model for its Sales and Customer Service systems in Dynamics 365.

Together we explored Copilot Studio and chatbot capabilities, including a Copilot Studio integration with Shopify that connects customer interaction tools with eCommerce systems. We also helped multiple Dynamics 365 applications and supporting technologies, including Dataverse and Azure-based infrastructure, work together effectively.

What changed day to day?

Compana runs a more stable, supported Sales and Customer Service environment and has a trusted partner for both day-to-day needs and new initiatives.
