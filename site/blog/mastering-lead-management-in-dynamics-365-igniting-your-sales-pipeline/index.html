The short answerDynamics 365 Sales manages a lead from first contact to a qualified opportunity. Leads from your website, social media and email campaigns land in one place. You score them, route them to the right seller, nurture them with automated follow-up, and convert qualified leads into opportunities. Reports then show which sources and nurturing steps work best.

Update, November 2026

What changed since this post was written?

The Microsoft features in this post were checked 10 November 2026 on Microsoft Learn. Dynamics 365 Sales now offers predictive lead scoring, assignment rules and an AI agent that researches and engages leads. Microsoft also recommends Power Automate flows instead of classic workflows for new automation.

The original post follows, with those features linked where it mentions them.

How do leads get into Dynamics 365?

Dynamics 365 collects leads from your website, social media, email campaigns and other sources in one central place.

Lead entry forms can be tailored to your business, so you gather the details your team needs from the start.

How do you qualify and score leads?

Start by setting the criteria that make a lead ready for sales, such as demographics, lead source and engagement level.

Lead scoring then gives each lead a number based on its attributes and interactions. A higher score means a better chance of converting, so sellers know where to start.

Microsoft’s predictive lead scoring builds that score from your own history. It needs at least 40 qualified and 40 disqualified leads to train a model (checked 10 November 2026).

How do you route leads to the right seller?

Assignment rules route each lead by geographic territory, product expertise and workload, so it reaches the right person fast.

Faster response times mean more chances to convert. In Dynamics 365 Sales, assignment rules assign new leads to sellers or teams automatically once a rule is active (checked 10 November 2026).

How do you nurture leads until they are ready to buy?

Automated follow-up sends personalized emails, newsletters and targeted promotions.

Dynamics 365 keeps each lead’s interaction and engagement history, so your team can tailor what it sends next. That might mean sharing more information, answering a concern or offering an exclusive deal.

For new automation, Microsoft recommends Power Automate flows rather than classic workflows (checked 10 November 2026).

What happens when a lead is qualified?

A qualified lead moves into the opportunity stage, where sellers build tailored solutions and proposals.

When you qualify a lead, Dynamics 365 can create the account, contact and opportunity for you. What it creates depends on how your admin set it up (checked 10 November 2026).

Which lead reports should you watch?

Track conversion rates, lead sources and how well each nurturing approach works.

Those numbers show where to put your budget and your people. The aim is more leads becoming opportunities, and a better return on what you spend to get them.

How do sales and marketing share leads in Dynamics 365?

Sales and marketing work in the same system, so leads move from marketing to sales without being re-entered.

Marketing automation brings leads in. Sellers then use the same lead data to close deals.

Frequently asked questions

How does lead scoring work in Dynamics 365?

Predictive lead scoring uses a machine learning model trained on your past leads to score open ones. It needs at least 40 qualified and 40 disqualified leads from the training period.

Can Dynamics 365 assign leads automatically?

Yes. Assignment rules in Dynamics 365 Sales assign new leads to sellers or teams based on conditions you set, such as territory or product.

What happens when you qualify a lead?

The lead closes as qualified. Dynamics 365 can then create an account, contact and opportunity from it, depending on how your admin set it up.
