How much does Dynamics 365 cost per user?

Each app is priced per user per month, paid yearly, except Customer Insights, which is priced per company. Most teams start with Sales Enterprise, Customer Service Enterprise, or both.

What else goes into the total cost?

Licenses are only part of the bill. Budget for storage, Copilot Credits if you run agents, the build itself, and someone to keep the system healthy after go-live.

Yes. enCloud9 sells Microsoft licenses. Ask about them on your call.

How much does Dynamics 365 cost per user?

At Microsoft list price, paid yearly, Dynamics 365 Sales runs $65 to $150 per user per month, Customer Service $50 to $195, Field Service $105 and Project Operations $135. Customer Insights is priced per company, from $1,000 a month with attach pricing.

What is the cheapest Dynamics 365 license?

For full users, Customer Service Professional and Field Service Contractor are the lowest listed prices at $50 per user per month. For light users, the Team Members license costs less; ask us for the current price.

Can I pay for Dynamics 365 monthly?

Yes. Microsoft's listed prices are for paying yearly. Paying monthly on an annual subscription costs 5% more, a rule Microsoft has applied since 1 April 2025.

Is there a minimum number of users?

Most apps have no minimum on Microsoft's pricing pages. Microsoft Relationship Sales needs 10 seats, and Customer Insights Attach needs 10 or more users of a qualifying Dynamics 365 app.

What does setup cost?

Accelerators are $7,500 fixed, a full implementation starts at $20,000, and Rescue and Repair runs $500 to $10,000, each quoted in writing. Microsoft licenses are a separate cost, and support plans start at $500 a month.
