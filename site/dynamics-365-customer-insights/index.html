Customer Insights is priced per company, not per user, so every marketer can use it. Attach pricing applies when you already have 10 or more users of a qualifying Dynamics 365 app.

What is Dynamics 365 Customer Insights?

Microsoft's marketing and customer data app. It joins customer data into one view of each person and runs email, text and push journeys on it.

How much does Customer Insights cost?

Customer Insights is $1,700 per company per month, or $1,000 with attach pricing, paid yearly. It includes 100,000 unified people and 10,000 interacted people, with unlimited users.

Who qualifies for attach pricing?

Companies with 10 or more users of a qualifying Dynamics 365 app, such as Sales or Customer Service.

Customer Insights or ClickDimensions?

Customer Insights suits teams that need one customer view across systems and reactive journeys. ClickDimensions suits simpler email marketing inside Dynamics 365. See marketing automation.

What does setup cost?

Sales and Service Accelerators are $7,500 fixed, a full implementation starts at $20,000, and Rescue and Repair runs $500 to $10,000, each quoted in writing. Microsoft licenses are a separate cost, and support plans start at $500 a month.
