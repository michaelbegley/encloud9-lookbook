About enCloud9

A Dynamics 365 partner with one way of working

It connects the rest with Power Apps, Power Automate and Power BI. enCloud9 also takes over failing Dynamics systems and gets them working again.

What do we believe about Dynamics 365 projects?

Nine plain rules we hold ourselves to on every project. If a line on this site breaks one of them, it should not be there.

  1. 01See the plan before we buildYou get the written plan: users, data, integrations and where Copilot fits, before any build work starts.
  2. 02Every change is written downAnything outside the package is a written change order you approve before work starts.
  3. 03Prices on the pageOur four support plans are published with their prices, so you know the cost after go-live.
  4. 04Standard features firstWe build with standard features first, because over-customized systems are the ones we most often get called in to rescue.
  5. 05One process, every projectPlan, Build, Go live and Run, in that order, whatever the size of the project.
  6. 06Say what is included and what is notEvery package lists both. Integrations with other systems are quoted separately.
  7. 07A number has a unit and a sourceIf we cannot show where a figure comes from, it does not go on the page.
  8. 08AI after a data and permissions reviewCopilot and agents go on top of a system that is ready, with the running cost written down first.
  9. 09The team that builds it supports itAfter go-live, the people who built your system keep it running.

How does an enCloud9 project run?

Every project runs through the same four stages: Plan, Build, Go live and Run. You see the written plan before we build, and every change after that is written down and approved.

What does support cost after go-live?

Support starts at $500 a month for 4 hours on the Stability plan, which is the minimum plan for new clients. Larger teams choose Optimization at $1,500 a month for 20 hours or Partnership from $2,000 a month for 35 hours.
