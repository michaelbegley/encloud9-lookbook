What are the four stages of a project?

Plan, Build, Go live and Run. You see the written plan before we build, and every change after that is written down and approved. The Schiff Group went live on a new Dynamics 365 Sales system in one month →

Stage APlanWe learn how your team works, using a written discovery questionnaire, and give you the plan: users, stages or queues, data to move, integrations, and where Copilot and agents fit.
Stage BBuildWe build Dynamics 365 to the plan, using standard features first, and move your data. You check the imported records against your old system before sign-off.
Stage CGo liveTraining and cut-over day. Copilot is switched on if your plan includes it; agents are scoped as a separate pilot.
Stage DRunYour support plan starts. Health checks and updates are logged, so you always know what changed.

What do you put in writing?

Everything that affects your budget or your timeline. You never find out about a change from an invoice.

What does support cost after go-live?

Support starts at $500 a month for 4 hours on the Stability plan, which is the minimum plan for new clients. Larger teams choose Optimization at $1,500 a month for 20 hours or Partnership from $2,000 a month for 35 hours.
