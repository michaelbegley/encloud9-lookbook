What does Dynamics 365 do for a distributor?

Distributors live on price, stock and credit. These are the jobs Dynamics 365 takes off the phone and out of spreadsheets.

When does a distributor need Dynamics 365?

When sellers call the office to check stock, price or credit before they can answer a customer.

A good fit if

  • Reps quote from an old price sheet, then find out the item is out of stock.
  • Accounting gets asked every day whether a customer is on credit hold.
  • Orders are typed twice, once in the CRM and once in the ERP.
  • Returns and questions live in one person's inbox.

Do you work with distributors?

Yes. enCloud9 builds Dynamics 365 for small and mid-sized businesses, and distributors are one of the industries we lead with.

Can our sellers see stock and credit in Dynamics 365?

Yes, if you run Business Central. Microsoft's integration shows item availability, price lists, balances and open invoices in Dynamics 365 Sales. See the Business Central integration.

What if we run a different ERP?

Tell us what you run and we will tell you what the link takes. Other connections can be built with Power Automate or custom work, quoted in writing.

Can Dynamics 365 handle returns?

Yes. Dynamics 365 Customer Service tracks each return or question as a case, with queues and service levels, so nothing sits in one inbox.

What does it cost?

Accelerators are $7,500 fixed, a full implementation starts at $20,000, and Rescue and Repair runs $500 to $10,000, each quoted in writing. Microsoft licenses are a separate cost, and support plans start at $500 a month.
