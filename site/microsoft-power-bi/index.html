When does a Dynamics 365 team need Power BI?

Dynamics 365 has its own dashboards, and for many teams they are enough. Power BI earns its license when you report across several systems, need heavy analysis, or share reports beyond CRM users.

What is Power BI?

Microsoft's reporting tool. It connects to Dynamics 365 and many other data sources and turns them into reports and dashboards in a browser or on a phone.

Do we need Power BI if Dynamics 365 has dashboards?

Not always. Dynamics 365 dashboards cover day-to-day pipeline and service views; we built them for BSI and REI. Power BI is for reporting across systems or heavier analysis.

How much does Power BI cost?

Power BI Pro is $14 per user per month and Premium Per User is $24, both paid yearly. A free account works for your own reports. Our build work is quoted in writing after a short call.

Who needs a Power BI license?

Anyone who shares reports, and anyone who views a shared report, needs Pro or Premium Per User, unless your company buys capacity.

Does Copilot replace Power BI?

No. Use Power BI for heavy analytics and Copilot for questions about your data. See Copilot and AI.
