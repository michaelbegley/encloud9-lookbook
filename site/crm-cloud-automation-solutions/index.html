Which package do I need?

Start from where your system is today: a first Dynamics 365 Sales or Customer Service system fits an accelerator, and a system that needs integrations or data from several systems on day one fits a full implementation. A Dynamics 365 system that is over-customized or stalled fits Rescue and Repair.

Are Microsoft licenses included?

No. Microsoft licenses and Copilot Credits are not part of the package price, and you pay for them separately. As part of setup we assign your licenses to each user and set their security roles, because Microsoft does not let a user open the app until both are done.

How long does a Dynamics 365 setup take?

Our accelerators take 4 weeks. A full implementation typically takes 8 to 16 weeks. You get the plan, with dates, before we build.

What if we need more than the package covers?

Anything outside the package is a written change order you approve before work starts. If you need integrations or data from several systems at go-live, a full implementation fits better.

Can you take over a system another partner built?

Yes. Rescue and Repair is for Dynamics 365 systems that are over-customized, stalled or out of date. We review it first and tell you what to keep.

Do we need a support plan after go-live?

Yes. New clients start on at least the Stability plan, $500 a month for 4 hours, on a 12-month agreement. Monthly plans include a 1-hour response during business hours, 7:00am to 5:00pm Central, Monday to Friday.
