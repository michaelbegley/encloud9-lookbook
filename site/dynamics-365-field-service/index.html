When does a business need Field Service?

When your work happens at the customer's site: installs, repairs, inspections or maintenance. Field Service connects the dispatcher, the technician and the office on one record.

What is Dynamics 365 Field Service?

Microsoft's app for on-site work: work orders, scheduling and dispatch, and a mobile app technicians use to see jobs and record the work.

How much does Field Service cost?

Field Service is $105 per user per month and Field Service Contractor is $50, both paid yearly. Automatic schedule planning is a $30 add-in per resource per month.

What does Copilot do in Field Service?

Copilot helps create and update work orders, schedule them, and summarize the work, so dispatchers and technicians spend less time typing.

Can cases from Customer Service become work orders?

Yes. Field Service runs on the same data as Customer Service, so a case can turn into a work order without retyping.

What does setup cost?

Our build work is a fixed price, quoted in writing after a short call. Microsoft licenses are a separate cost, and support plans start at $500 a month.
