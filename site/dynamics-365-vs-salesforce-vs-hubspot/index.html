Which one fits which team?

All three are serious CRMs. The right one is the one that fits how your business already works, and that your team will use every day.

A good rule of thumb

  • Dynamics 365 fits teams that live in Outlook and Teams and want the CRM, apps, reports and AI on one Microsoft platform.
  • Salesforce fits teams already invested in Salesforce and its ecosystem.
  • HubSpot fits small teams that want to start free with marketing and sales in one tool.

Is Dynamics 365 cheaper than Salesforce?

At list price, Dynamics 365 Sales runs $65 to $150 per user per month and Salesforce Sales Cloud $25 to $550. The plans differ in what they include, so compare the plan you would actually need, plus AI and setup.

Is HubSpot free?

HubSpot's Free plan is free for up to 2 users. Paid Sales Hub plans start from $7 per seat per month, and Professional and Enterprise add a one-time onboarding fee of $1,500 or $3,500.

Which CRM works best with Microsoft 365?

Dynamics 365 works inside Outlook and Teams and runs on the same platform as Power Apps and Power BI. Sales Enterprise and Premium include Copilot in Dynamics 365 Sales, Outlook and Teams.

When is Dynamics 365 the wrong choice?

When a very small team wants a free start with no Microsoft 365 in the business, or when a company is deeply invested in another platform and happy with it. We will tell you if that is you.
